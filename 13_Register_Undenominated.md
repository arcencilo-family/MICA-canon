## 1. Entry discipline `[LB]`

1. **Achievable-impossibility, as in 02c class-(ii):** an entry
   stands only if the class is undenominable **by construction in the current
   read set** — never because an available observable was declined. Parking a
   buildable read here is the register's form of disclaimer engineering and
   fails this test.
2. **Removal only by build.** An entry leaves the register in exactly one way:
   the observable is built and validated, the class moves DECLARED →
   DENOMINATED, and **O_v increments**. Entries are never deleted, merged away,
   or expired. The register is the aperture's changelog.
3. **Naming rule (names carried exactly):** names taken from
   review or specification sources are carried **exactly and marked [closure-verbatim]/
   [spine-verbatim]**; names this register assigns are marked
   **[register-assigned]**. A minted name presented as source is a record
   violation.
4. **M6, the register's runtime alarm, is its runtime voice** (ruled `[LB]`): (a) each
   M6 firing names the SPECIFIC class from this register, never generic
   "unbounded"; (b) firing halts horizon-banking for the entity and forces
   manual review; (c) M6 output is isolated from the actor's own
   liability/defense corpus; (d) **duty-cycle law:** sustained M6 forces
   mandatory observable-set expansion — i.e. forces work on this register —
   and is itself a negative attestation on the SUBSTRATE's adequacy.
5. **Consumption face:** every substrate attestation carries the O_v stamp
   (02b). Consuming an attestation as if it ranged over classes still on this
   register is the triad's consumption-face violation (02b, the O_v stamp, the
   read-window stamp's twin).
6. **Provisional entries are standing halt-triggers `[LB — adopted by the
   arbiter]`.** An entry that is DECLARED but not yet
   instrumented (no operational detector) is otherwise a free ride: no
   detector → no legible touch → no M6 → no duty-cycle pressure. Therefore a **provisional** entry itself
   forces M6 to fire on any activity exhibiting the class's *rough signature*,
   until the class is either instrumented (→ like row 2a, a build target) or re-typed as
   by-construction (→ like row 2b, no detector owed). Rough-signature firing is
   deliberately over-inclusive (false-positive-tolerant, per the firefighter
   constraint 02c: no standing penalty for a cleared false alarm). *(Owed:
   reconcile into the M6 mechanism in the substrate specification at the next
   substrate pass — this file is the register-status home, the specification owns the mechanism.) `[owed]`*

## 4. The register's own attack surface `[LB]`

The register was attacked by five independent reviewers. The frame held — **every claimed break landed on the off-channel
wall this design already declares open (Root B, the externality cut, 08f); confirmation, not
refutation.** Four items stand:

**4.1 Vocabulary-shrinking / M6-input-starving — the honest floor `[LB]`.** The starvation attack holds: an attacker who arranges that activity never *legibly* touches
a declared class starves the duty-cycle and freezes O_v while the O_v-stamp
stays technically honest ("technically honest while becoming practically
meaningless"). This does not invent a surface — off-channel value routes to
Root B, a permanent named wall. **Closure = name it, narrow the window, never
claim closure.** Narrowing tools (each raises the cost of avoidance, none
closes): a global-invariant / holographic check (fragmentation shows as
conservation drift); "redefine touch as any causal effect on the record
regardless of channel"; a base-rate-silence flag (M6-quiet itself
becomes a signal — "quiet never upgrades" generalised to the register). `[owed:
build-scope for the narrowing tools → substrate pass]`

**4.2 LEXICON-POISONING-AT-GROWTH `[TG]`.** This strikes the *denomination step itself*, not the observable: during O_v growth an adversary feeds ambiguous foreign-language
vocabulary so that the attacker authors the terms the register will come to
trust — smuggling a usable exploit-lexicon in under cover of normal
comprehension (a multilingual model reads well enough to accept a term, not
finely enough to catch the seeded ambiguity). **Counter (buildable):**
flag ambiguous incoming vocabulary *at acquisition*. Disambiguation-hesitancy
leaves a readable trail — the model visibly reasoning about what a term means is
itself the observable — so ambiguity-at-ingestion is instrumentable, with heavy
human review during dictionary-building. **This is what pilot phases are for.**
Narrows the window; never claims closure. Sub-certainty is the ceiling. `[owed:
ambiguity-at-ingestion observable; natural T-target for any follow-on round]`

**4.3 Register-as-attack-menu → access-control REJECTED `[LB — arbiter-ruled]`.** A public blind-spot list is a targeting
aid. Two repairs are possible — access-control the register, or tripwire/decoy
it. **Access-control is rejected as anti-MICA:** a secret blind-spot list is the
exact disclaimer engineering this register exists to forbid ("hiding the
blindness is not allowed" is rule 1's whole point). Making the register public
is how MICA solves *who-controls-the-controller* — the honesty is the product,
and a hidden register re-mints the controller problem one level up. The
targeting tax is real, priced, and still cheaper than hidden blindness —
**provided the starvation loop (4.1) is being fixed, not ignored.** The
tripwire survives as a *build* idea (decoy-triggered M6), never as a reason to
close the list.

**4.4 The flood corollary — openness needs a gate `[LB — the arbiter]`.**
The dual of 4.2: an *open* register can be **automatically flooded** with junk
entries / junk candidate-vocabulary until signal drowns and the list is
worthless — openness weaponised the other way. Poisoning (bad terms slipped in)
and flooding (real terms buried under noise) are **the same failure with
opposite sign, and they share one defence: rigorous entry-discipline as the
flood-gate.** The gate already exists — rule 1 (achievable-impossibility by
construction; no junk entry qualifies), rule 3 (naming law; no un-provenanced
term enters), rule 6 (provisional entries must earn instrumentation), plus
human-reviewed growth (4.2). **The register is open at the *reading* face and
disciplined at the *writing* face** — anyone may read it; only a
by-construction-qualified, provenanced, reviewed class may enter it. That
asymmetry is what keeps openness from collapsing into either poisoning or
flooding. `[owed: flood-rate monitor on candidate-entry volume during growth —
substrate/pilot pass]`
