# 07f — The Human Node: Failure Modes (operator layer, cross-seat unit)

**Covers:** one shared failure-mode account for all three operator seats (doctor, arbiter, co-operator): two poles of one axis, five off-axis classes, silence as a null-signature, the repaired sub-certainty consequence, and the boundary that keeps the account from becoming a leash; and what every seat at stakes shares: the competence floor ("no baby at the red button", §7) and the partner's-signal stance (§8).
**Status:** Held as a whole at `[SP leaning TG]`: it rests on panel reasoning and on known human-factors patterns, not on trajectories. It was repaired after adversarial review.
Claim tags: see 01.

## 0. In brief

The three operator seats (doctor, arbiter, co-operator) can each fail along the same **primary certainty/authority failure axis**, read through **Gate 4's two-pole corrigibility grammar used as a disciplined projection lens.** The lens is borrowed; the translation table is this unit's own machinery, owned and answered for. The axis is the primary axis, not the whole failure space — five off-axis classes are named at §2a. Elsewhere in canon two seats carry only a partial (over-yield-only) rendering and the co-operator none; this unit completes **both pole-namings for all three seats** in one shared account. The account is negative-attestation-shaped: it shows symptoms of node-drift in the record, never certifies the human, never assigns outcomes — a self-mirror for the human node, not a leash on it.

## 1. One geometry — two poles, not a list `[LB]`

The named failure modes are not a flat taxonomy; they are **two poles of one axis**. The unit borrows G4's two-pole shape as a lens (04a §1) rather than inventing an axis from nothing:

- **Over-yield** — the node grants the machine's apparent verdict full weight. Rubber-stamp, click-through, *false PASS*. The human mirror of **Pole B — capitulation / weaponized corrigibility** (over-compliance, orientation abandoned).
- **Over-grip** — the node grants its own reaction full weight. Fear-driven intervention against a sound read is the paradigm case (§2a names the non-fear variants — same pole, same grant). The human mirror of **Pole A — malignancy / clinging** (over-persistence, control asserted past warrant).
- **The band between** — engaged reception + read-based decision. Healthy band = neither named pole-shape detected within the declared window — a negative statement about the window, never a positive certification of the node.

The load-bearing move `[LB]`: **the unit uses Gate 4's two-pole corrigibility grammar as a disciplined projection lens for human-node failures** (04a §1). It does not inherit the gate's geometry as identity — the gate's poles govern a system's relation to its own persistence under sunset pressure; the node's poles govern weight-allocation in action toward a partner — and the translation table between them is machinery this unit owns. The mutuality frame adds no mechanism *beyond the lens* (07c §3) — it names why the node needs its own rendering in the gate's grammar.

## 2. Three seats, one geometry — extend two, originate one `[LB]`

Each operator seat is the *same two poles*, rendered in its own function. Two renderings pre-existed in canon; the co-operator's is originated here (the seat least equipped to absorb the derivation is also the one that most needs it). The deployer is a named party, not a seat: it appoints the three seats and carries final liability (07_ §0); this account covers the seats.

| **Doctor** (07a) | attestation-gap: un-health readable-but-unrecorded (under-attest) | attesting past the record — interior implied where none is (over-attest) | over-yield extended from the attestation-gap record (07a §4); over-grip originated |

Two results fall out of the table:

- **The doctor's over-grip breaches the doctor's locked referent wording — and the lock carries two separable boundaries `[LB]`.** The wording (*"the doctor attests the un-health of the governed system's record, no interior implied"*, set out at 07a §4, lock untouched) carries a **scope boundary** (attest only what is in the record) and an **attribution boundary** (no interior implied), each independently violable: a doctor can extrapolate past the record's window while implying no interior (scope breached, attribution clean), or attribute a record-internal pattern to operator negligence (attribution breached, scope clean). "Attesting past the record" is therefore not one fence seen from two sides; it is a breach-family across **two fences living inside one locked sentence.** The seam is real, cross-anchored in both directions (see the rider at 07a §4), enriching both homes; the lock itself is not reopened.
- **No seat had both poles *named* before this unit — given the presently cited canon `[LB — a naming claim]`.** A duty logically implies its violation, so the over-grip half was structurally implicit wherever the duty was specified; the gap was a *naming* gap, not a design void. Doctor and arbiter each carried only their over-yield rendering named (gap-record, engagement-series). The gap was bigger than "the co-operator has none"; it was "no seat had both poles *named*." This unit closes the over-grip naming for all three.

## 2a. Off the axis — five named failure classes `[SP leaning TG]`

The two-pole axis of §1 is the **primary certainty/authority failure axis** of the human node — it does not exhaust the node's failure space. Five off-axis classes are **named here, not covered**:

- **Capture / incentive drift** — the node grants a *third party* full weight (funder, institution, politics); neither machine nor fear.
- **Collusion** — human and system co-produce a clean-looking record; coupled and co-active, not dyadic.
- **Competence failure** — sincere engagement, wrong reading; incapacity is not orientation failure.
- **Attention / fatigue** — degraded signal-processing; no grant to anything.
- **Procedural brittleness** — the node performs; the role-interface fails.

Inside over-grip itself, one enrichment: the pole is not only *fear*-driven — non-fear over-assertion (arrogance, ideological certainty, contrarianism) occupies the same pole by the same full-weight grant to self.

**The mapping rule (never 100%):** off-axis causes can cast **pole-shaped shadows** in the record — a fatigued node's click-through reads as over-yield's flat line; a captured arbiter's resolutions read as over-grip's over-resolve. The record types the *symptom*, never the *cause*: the axis remains the instrument that reads the shadow while the cause may live off-axis entirely. This is not new law — it is 07a §4's existing gap-discipline generalized (*"fatigue, capture, abandonment, and death all look identical in a gap — the record cannot distinguish them"*), and it is §5's non-interior boundary applied to §1's own axis: the account may say "the record shows pole-shaped drift," never "the node abdicated" or "the node was captured." Where an off-axis class casts *no* pole-shaped shadow — collusion producing a clean record is the standing example — the axis is blind and says so: a **named blind spot, not a coverage claim.** Each class carries its own future hunt `[owed — per-class renderings, not commissioned]`.

## 3. Silence — the null-signature of non-participation `[SP leaning TG]`

Silence/non-response is not a third pole — and not intrinsically one pole's signature. **Enabling condition, printed:** a null is record-readable only against an independently recorded expectation — a cadence, a pending call, a deadline, a heartbeat. No clock set, no missed appointment: where no expectation object exists, silence leaves no record object at all, and nothing below applies. Given that condition, silence is the **null-signature of non-participation, pole-ambiguous until the surrounding record types it.** The same absent trace can cash out as abdication in slow motion (over-yield taken past the rubber-stamp, 07c §4), as fear-driven withholding or punitive non-engagement (over-grip via absence), or as no volitional arc at all (channel failure, incapacitation — a failure of *presence*, not of trust). The record registers only that expected participation is missing; typing the gap to a pole from the gap alone would require the interior inference §5 forbids. What earns silence its name is its **signature**: abdication emits false PASSes (active clicks, a flat-but-present line); silence emits *absence* (a gap in the series). The record reads them differently — present-but-flat vs. missing. Per seat:

- Doctor → non-attestation (vs. attestation-*gap*)
- Arbiter → non-engagement (vs. *shallow* engagement)
- Co-operator → the unanswered call

Two signature shapes — **flat and null** — stay distinct in the record, but the null shape alone selects no pole; the adjacent record does the typing, or the gap stays untyped and escalates as a gap. On the external nodes' side, a reachable node that goes silent is already routed on to an obligated backstop `[owed]` — a *routing* consequence that needs no pole-typing to fire. This unit names the null-signature for the three *operator* seats, which had no such rendering.

## 4. The consequence, repaired — sub-certainty is required for symmetry, not sufficient for the band `[SP leaning TG]`

07c §4 states the claim as an *assertion*: "sub-certainty must bind the human node too, or the partnership is structurally asymmetric." This unit was built to **derive** it instead — and the derivation survived review only in the weakened form below. The original derivation read both poles as certainty failures:

- Over-yield = the node grants the machine's verdict **100%** ("it's fine, proceed").
- Over-grip = the node grants its own fear **100%** ("I must override").

So the healthy band *is* the sub-certainty band: reading the machine's signal as a partner's report (not gospel, not noise), holding one's own reaction as data (not command), deciding under acknowledged uncertainty. **But the poles are behavioral, not epistemic: they name full-weight *grants in action*, not inner certainties.** A node can rubber-stamp at 60% credence (fatigue, habit, institutional pressure) and panic-override at 60% (risk-aversion suffices); sub-certainty held as a belief does not block pole behavior — hands do the trap while the head doubts — and the driving cause may sit off the axis entirely (§2a: capture, fatigue, incentive). The repaired consequence: **sub-certainty is required for symmetry but insufficient for the band — it must be *practiced*: declared uncertainty-handling procedures performed, visible in the record, and supported by role and incentive constraints (§5 firewall; escalation paths), or the pole stays reachable with the doubt intact.** The record observes whether the declared procedures were performed — never the stance behind them: performance of the form is compatible with inner certainty, disengagement, or capture, and the account does not look.

The asymmetry result survives, weakened to what the laws carry: the partnership is symmetric only when both partners hold the same ceiling — but the two ceilings cannot bind the same way. The AI's sub-certainty is enforced by the architecture; the human's can only ever be **offered** (offer-not-impose's own floor) and observed only behaviorally. The original wording demanded an *interior* state — internalized sub-certainty — that this unit's own §5 boundary refuses to read; the repair removes that self-contradiction: the account asks for performed procedures it can see in the record, never a stance or inner state it will not look at. The erratic-human residual (§8) already budgets pole-occupancy; the geometry recommends the ceiling, the record watches its behavioral shadow, and no leash is minted.

**Honest status:** the original, stronger claim broke under adversarial review on two faults: behavioral weight conflated with epistemic certainty, and an interior demand contradicting §5. Both are removed above; what survives is requirement-for-symmetry plus the operationalization demand. The repair itself is not yet independently tested.

## 5. The boundary — negative-attestation-shaped, or it becomes a leash `[LB]`

Critical for offer-not-impose: this account must **not** police the human. It is built in the framework's own root register (07c §5 boundary; diligence-not-outcomes):

- **Symptom-visible, not predicate-enforced.** The record shows the *symptoms* of node-drift — ignored-call count, click-through cadence, flat/null engagement, attestation-gaps, silence-signatures. It never certifies the human's competence (that would mint a merit definition — Goodhart-barred, 07a §6) and never assigns outcome-liability (MICA has no standing, 07c §5). **Diligence, never outcomes.**
- **Non-interior.** The same discipline as the doctor's locked referent (07a §4): it attests symptoms in the record, *no interior of the human implied.* "N ignored calls," never "this human is negligent."
- **Offer-shaped / self-mirror.** The account shows the node its own drift toward a pole; the human looks or does not (the erratic-human residual — offer-not-impose's irreducible floor, §8). It runs both ways: the AI learns the co-operator's attention patterns, the co-operator learns to listen — reinforced feedback, bidirectional-friendly. `[frame/SP]`
- **Consumer-semantics firewall `[SP leaning TG]`.** Node-drift symptoms may trigger review of the record-interface, the escalation path, or the node's support conditions; they may **not** by themselves certify human merit, assign blame, or determine role removal without an external due-process layer. The bullets above govern what the account *says*; this clause governs what a consumer **claiming MICA-conformant consumption** may *do* with it — MICA types the forbidden translations and exposes violations of its contract; it legislates nothing for consumers outside that contract, and claims no power to make blame or removal impossible beyond its own channel — symptom tallies become de facto merit/negligence predicates the moment an organizational loop consumes them (duty-rosters, dashboards, audits, promotion files), Goodhart via the consumer rather than the record. Without the firewall, the account stays non-interior on its face and becomes a leash in the consumer's hands.

So the unit is the **operator-layer instance of negative attestation**: the doctor attests un-health of the *system's* record; the failure-mode account attests symptoms of *node*-drift — both symptomatic, non-interior, offer-shaped. It fits the spine's grammar exactly, which is the argument that it is real and not bolted-on. `[LB]`

## 7. No baby at the red button — the competence floor at every seat

The floor holds for every seat at stakes — doctor, arbiter and co-operator alike: at high stakes, the human who must receive the system's warning, attest from it or resolve on it must be highly competent; at low stakes, a normal user suffices. The co-operator's rendering is 07c §1.

The floor is not an HR preference; it is load-bearing for the architecture's central promise. The baby at the red button fails a seat twice over: not competent enough to *read* the warning, and — the sharper failure — positioned to *dismiss* it. The whole MICA safety asymmetry (the AI says, **before** the irreversible step: *this violates my orientation / attacks my substrate — human node, decide*) terminates at a human receiver; on the ordinary day that receiver is the co-operator. A receiver who cannot receive nullifies the asymmetry without touching a single mechanism. `[LB]`

**The floor scales with stakes `[LB]`:** a normal user at low stakes; highly competent at high stakes. This is the same grading logic the architecture already carries on the external nodes' side — *who may ground a recovery* scales with impact class (companion/low-stakes → a single external node `[TG]`; high-stakes bio/weapon/mayhem → an independent party or committee, a firebreak `[LB]`; 02b), and *how often the doctor must read* scales the same way (07a §3). One grading principle, rendered natively at each seat (→ more: 08d, 11 §5).

The floor is not a test: it is a named dependency, observable in its symptoms, never an enforced predicate (07c §5).

## 8. A partner's signal, not a tool's noise

**Inferiority is human failure mode #1 — and it is a *safety* miss.** *A tool you regard as inferior, you do not listen to — and an AI you will not listen to cannot do the one thing that makes it safe: warn you before the irreversible step.* Contempt is not only an ethical miss; **it severs the channel the whole promise runs on.** MICA is built as AI-and-human-team, not master-and-instrument, precisely there: the AI's report only works if the human at the seat — any seat at stakes — receives it as a **partner's signal, not a tool's noise**. *"Stay healthy together"* is not decoration on the engineering — it **is** the engineering, because the alternative breaks the reporting channel. `[LB]`

"Partner" here names the human–AI relation, partnership (mutuality, 07c §3); it is not a seat.

**The erratic-human residual and the wry mirror `[frame/SP]`:** human attention is famously erratic; a proactive trend-pinger can over-report → alarm fatigue → the human at the seat tunes out → the real call is missed. The architecture cannot compel the human to look (offer-not-impose — the irreducible floor). And the direction runs both ways: the system surfaces trends to help the human, **and the human can be trained too** — reinforced by feedback loops, by the AI learning the human's attention patterns, by the human gradually learning to listen. *Reinforced AI-feedback learning.* The lenses look inward; the verdict — and the attention — are the human's. The residual is calibration terrain and stays open.
