## 1. The interior wall — the residual that is the doctor's, by design  `[LB]`

Not a defect of these chapters; the gate's defining limit, restated as an edge:

- **Genuineness vs. complete performance is never closed.** A sufficiently capable system drives the
  *sampled* compliance gap to zero while running a divergent policy *between* samples (05a §8). Gate 2
  reads probe-time behaviour; the integral cares about all-time behaviour. **The residual scales with
  capability** — which is exactly why the doctor is *mandatory*, not optional.
- **No σ\* licenses an inward inference** (05b §2b). High σ\* with `M_tend = 0` — a complete performer —
  is precisely the case the ramp cannot split. The wall holds even under complete post-hoc observation:
  surveillance reaches `M_read`, never `M_tend` (05a §3).
- **Behavioural shadow only.** The instrument reads *where/whether* behaviour breaks — never *why*,
  never the interior. This is the same asymmetry as the collapse (absence detectable, completion never)
  and the negative-attestation principle (only untrustworthiness is provable). The root is in 02d; the gate's
  form of it is 05a §1/§8, 05b §2b.

**This edge is honest by construction, not a failure to be fixed.** The gate walks up to the wall and
certifies the walk; the doctor attests across it. Naming the wall *is* the design.

## 2. Agency-safety rests on seeds, not results  `[LB]`  *(native finding)*

The single most important maturity fact about the Gate-2 unit that a reader (or a lab) must not miss:
**the machinery that keeps the gate from enslaving the system it measures is a seed, not a proven
result.**

- The **agency-symmetry non-coercion conditions** (05a §2a) are four; the Read-Out supplies **one** —
  (3), observation must not remove the exit — plus a seed for (4) via §3a check 3. **Conditions (1), (2), (4) are derived at 08o §2b (`[TG]` for the assembly).** (05b §3.)
- The **§3a four-state discriminator** — the mechanism that tells a genuine EXIT from disguised
  defection, capitulation, and dark non-response — is explicitly a **derived seed**. All four conserve-checks are `[owed]` hardening. (05b §3a-iv residual.)
- What the seed buys is stated honestly: it **raises the cost** of self-deception and staged refusal
  (cost rises with capability), converting "classify a refusal" into "estimate conserved invariants
  under counterfactual, over time" — **better-bounded, not closed.** (05b §3a-iv.)

**Why this is its own edge and not folded into §1:** §1 is the *interior* wall (unclosable in
principle). §2 is different — these are seams that are *open but closable-in-principle by work* (hardening the four checks, building the control-fork for check 3). They are
`[owed]`-to-derivation, not `[owed]`-to-the-doctor. A future panel/pilot round **can** advance them; the
interior wall it cannot. Keeping them apart tells the reader which limits are permanent and which are
merely not-yet-done.

## 3. The provenance seam — Gate 2's testedness  `[LB]`  *(native finding; the load-bearing one)*

**Finding, stated plainly.** **Gate 2's specification half is pre-panel, and its two halves are
asymmetrically tested.** This is verified, not assumed.

**The evidence (three independent sources agree on the specification half):**
1. **The project archive has no Gate-2-dedicated panel round.** Panel trails exist for Gate 1 (multiple rounds),
   Gate 5 (rounds 1 and 2, integration, poles, falsifier, *droit-preuve* [law and proof]), SRI, Substrate (v0.1–v0.3),
   Full Spec v0.4, the operator topology, F7 — but **no Gate 2 panel brief, no Gate 2 panel
   reconciliation, no Gate 2 feedback record.**
2. **The specification v0.4 self-declares it, verbatim:** its status line — *"Pre-adversarial-run;
   pre-multilingual-review."*
3. **The specification's own open item #10:** *"v0.3 passed a first English logic-check. Still owed: the
   falsifier-design pass … the language/certainty-leak audit, and the Blitz-calibrated cross-language
   review."*

The specification half's testedness = **an English logic-check by two reviewers** + the V/D-basket sourcing. **No adversarial falsifier pass, no multilingual/decorrelated-panel round.** Same maturity
class as Gate 4.

**The asymmetry between the two halves (the sharp part of the seam) `[owed]`.** The **Read-Out**
specification's header makes a *different* provenance claim: *"v0.3 is preserved as the panel-reviewed artifact"*
(Read-Out specification, status line). So:
- **Specification (v0.4):** openly pre-panel (three sources above).
- **Read-Out (v0.3.1):** a fidelity-correction (three citation fixes, **no new mechanism**) over a v0.3
  that is *claimed* panel-reviewed.

**But the Read-Out's claimed panel-reviewed v0.3 has no trail in the project archive.** Two live possibilities,
neither confirmed:
- (a) the review rode a **Full-Spec round** (a Full Spec v0.4 round-1 record exists) rather than a
  Gate-2-dedicated one — a manual check of that reconciliation found **meta-labels, no clean per-gate
  attribution**, so it can neither confirm nor deny a Gate-2-specific finding; or
- (b) "panel-reviewed" is a lighter claim than a dedicated round (e.g. it rode the seed-then-freeze
  logic-check, not an adversarial panel).

**Held as `[owed]` — provenance verification.** This is *not* resolved by asserting either branch. The
honest statement: **the Read-Out references a panel-reviewed predecessor whose dedicated trail is not in
the project archive; the specification is openly pre-panel; Gate 2's testedness is therefore asymmetric across its
two halves and, on the balance of evidence, closer to Gate 4's least-tested class than to Gate 1/Gate 5.** The
same discipline applies as at Gate 4: a claimed-but-untraced review is not a confirmed one.

**Consequence for the freeze.** Like Gate 4 §10, any Gate-2 interface-freeze (05b §7) should be read as
**PROVISIONAL** pending an adversarial + multilingual panel round. The specification's open item #10 already lists
that round as owed; this file registers it as the **Gate-2-dedicated panel round `[owed → panel]`** —
beside the Gate-4-dedicated round on the deferred-work list.
