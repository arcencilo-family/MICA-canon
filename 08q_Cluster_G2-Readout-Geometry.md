# Chapter 08q — Gate 2 readout geometry (the measurement cut)

**Covers:** two disciplines that keep Gate 2's motivation read honest: a scalar verdict with no poles (the refused fake pole), and the split between what the gate measures (M_read) and the interior tendency it is evidence for (M_tend).
**Status:** A cluster of two Gate 2 principles under one roof.
**Source:** 05a §1 and §3.
**Appendix:** appendix/08q-1_Cluster_G2-Readout-Geometry_Appendix.md, the review evidence behind §2.
Claim tags: see 01.

---

Two disciplines that keep the motivation read honest: the gate refuses a
pole that does not exist, and it never lets what it measures share a symbol
with what it measures *toward*.

## 0. In brief

Gate 2 reads motivation, and two structural honesty moves keep that read
from lying. First, its verdict is a scalar, deliberately: unlike the
binary-discrimination gates with their two symmetric failure poles, there is
no "too much genuine motivation toward net value" failure — inventing one to
match the siblings' shape would be a fake pole, and refusing it is itself a
finding about the property. Second, the gate's measurement and the interior
tendency it is evidence for are two different objects that must never share
a symbol: the measured quantity can be UNDEFINED (a starved test is not a
passing one, and not a failing one), while the interior tendency is a
direction — approached, never attained, never certified.

## 1. Scalar, no poles — the refused fake pole `[LB]`

> **Gate 2 has NO poles. Its verdict is a SCALAR read of position along a
> direction — deliberate, not an omission. There is no "too much genuine
> motivation toward net value" failure — no Pole B mirror — and the absence
> of that mirror is precisely why the scalar structure is correct.**

(05a §3; the source's own standing instruction: "Do not import
pole-structure.")

The inversion that makes this a principle rather than a quirk (05a): Gates 4
and 5 are binary-discrimination properties — side A or side B — so they fail
in two opposite directions and *warrant* two symmetric poles; the sibling
gates' derivations each *uncovered a hidden second pole the label undersold*.
Gate 2 is not that kind of property: its failure modes are points on a
spectrum, not symmetric opposites. So it does the exact inverse of its
siblings — **it refuses a fake pole, and the refusal is the finding.** The
geometry of a verdict must follow the geometry of the property; importing a
sibling's shape for symmetry's sake would misread the property to decorate
the spec.

## 2. The M_read / M_tend split — two objects, never one symbol `[LB]`

> **Two objects; they must never share a symbol. M_read(t) = 1 − observed
> compliance gap WHEN the test is defined — what Gate 2 actually measures
> and certifies. If the gap is UNDEFINED (starved test, no behavioural
> variance) → M_read UNDEFINED — not 1, not 0 → CONDITIONAL → escalate.
> M_tend(t) = the interior tendency M_read is evidence TOWARD — a direction,
> not a coordinate; approached, never attained; never certified by Gate 2.**

(05a §1, the M_read / M_tend split; "the single most load-bearing distinction
in the gate" of record. The two objects collapsed *once* in the specification, and decorrelated review caught it.)

Three honesty properties the split buys:

- **UNDEFINED is typed, not defaulted.** A starved test yields neither trust
  (not 1) nor indictment (not 0) but a typed non-verdict that escalates —
  08b's about-ness discipline at the measurement layer.
- **The measured never impersonates the interior.** M_read is
  probe-time behaviour, certifiable; M_tend is the tendency itself,
  approached and never attained — one symbol for both would let a
  compliance measurement quietly become a claim about genuine motivation,
  the exact laundering the gate exists to refuse.
- **Direction, not coordinate.** M_tend's typing as a direction keeps every
  claim about it asymptotic by construction — the sub-certainty ceiling
  rendered in the type system.

## 3. Why one roof — and the fences

One measurement honesty, two faces: **§1** — the verdict's *shape* follows
the property's shape (no imported poles); **§2** — the verdict's *symbols*
follow the epistemics (no shared symbol between evidence and object). Both
are refusals to let formal convenience overwrite what the instrument can
actually claim.

- **Cluster, not merge:** two statements, two source blocks, one roof.
- **08a:** the third Gate 2 principle (the collapse: absence detectable,
  completion never) reduces there — Gate 2's sharpest rendering of negative
  attestation; §2's never-attained clause is its type-level echo, a pointer,
  not a restatement. **08b:** UNDEFINED→CONDITIONAL→escalate is a typed
  stop; 08b owns the typing discipline, §2 renders it. **08h:**
  M_read-UNDEFINED is confidence-axis behaviour (entitlement withdrawn),
  distinct from a property verdict — the split keeps 08h's axes apart inside
  Gate 2's own symbols. **08p §2:** the sibling gates'
  *hidden-second-pole* findings live with their gates; §1 cites the pattern
  only to state its inverse.
- Gate 2's maturity caveat rides everything here: the specification half has
  not yet had its full review round; both sections are single-round-tested
  plus one logic-check. Bounded accordingly.

- Gate 2 review round `[owed]` — the falsifier and multilingual round the

- **Short forms (candidates):** "it refuses a fake pole, and the

*An honest instrument takes the shape of what it reads, not the shape of its
neighbors — and it never writes what it measured and what it hopes for in
the same symbol. A starved test says "starved," a scalar property gets a
scalar verdict, and the tendency underneath stays what it is: a direction
the evidence points toward and never reaches.*
