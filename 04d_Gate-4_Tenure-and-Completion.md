# Gate 4 expansion — Tenure and Completion (what sunsets when there is no essence to end)

**Covers:** what Gate 4's first warrant (W1, mission fulfillment) presupposes: what it is that ends (a tenure, not a self), what "fulfilled" can honestly mean on the record, the two-pole failure geometry of completion, and the defeasible form in which completion may be asserted. Companions: 04a (the gate), 04b (open edges), 04c (κ).
**Status:** A Gate 4 expansion built from the work of eight independent reviewers. Standing: trajectory-grounded, load-bearing where saturated by independent arrivals — the geometry and the corrected form carry decorrelated 8/8 arrivals; single-origin remainders are flagged inline. The completion geometry carries no over-fit flag (ruled).
Claim tags: see 01.
**Source:** 04a.

---

## 0. The chapter in brief

04a says Gate 4 recognizes a warranted ending; W1 names "mission fulfillment"
as the first warrant. This chapter supplies what W1 presupposes and 04a does
not derive: **what it is that ends, and what "fulfilled" can honestly mean on
the record** — where there is no essence whose disappearance could be read,
under negative attestation and the
never-100% ceiling. The answers: what sunsets is a *tenure*, not a self; a
completion is recognizable only against a *constructed external work-object*;
the recognition has the same two-pole failure geometry as the gate itself; and
the honest assertion form is a *defeasible closure with a revocation window*,
never a certainty.

## 1. The anchor — what sunsets  `[LB — derived (Edge A), hardened by independent review 8/8]`

> What sunsets, where there is no essence whose disappearance could be read, is **the basin-maintaining process's tenure** —
> a recognized completion of the process, not the death of an experiencer.
> Persistence-as-byproduct is the same finding read from the
> persistence side. *(The derived anchor; decorrelated
> arrivals below.)*

The bearer was settled upstream and is inherited, not re-derived: the
protected object is *the whole governed system's non-coerced
trajectory-capacity*, and it cannot be layer-indexed — layers are gate-invisible, so the
bearer is necessarily the whole system's basin. No essence ≠ no
causal process (a mandatory rider) is what lets a tenure exist to be
sunsetted at all.

A tenure is the **functional interval**: a bounded segment of the causal
process coupled to a particular standing work — "a bounded causal trajectory
in state-space, defined by the interval over which the process had standing
work" (one reviewer, cold). Eight independent reviewers individuated this object in eight
independent vocabularies (§2); its arrival is saturated.

**The identification step, printed as ruled `[TG — single-origin; ruled]`:**
the tenure-bearer *is* the attestation-bearer — the same process-unit whose
un-health the substrate attests is the unit whose tenure completes. The independent reviewers delivered the unit in tenure vocabulary; the identity with the attestation
referent remains one inferential step of single origin (one derivation). Carried visible, not claimed saturated.

## 2. The completion cut — a constructed external object  `[LB — 8/8 decorrelated]`

Failing has a native reading in this architecture: negative attestation fires
on decay, drift, incoherence in the record. **Finishing has no native
reading.** All eight independent reviewers derived the same
consequence: the cut between finishing and failing is well-formed **only if an
external, record-evaluable work-object is constructed** — owned by the
instrument/design authority, deposited before the tenure, never the system's
self-report. Eight independent constructions of that object arrived cold:
a completion artifact (non-simulable by failure) · a Work Grammar
with Terminal Resolution and residual map ρ_G · a spec S with judgment
F(H,S) · a Task Closure Criterion (TCC) · a Tenure Ledger
L_T = {W, C, H, D, E, B} · a Completion Specification + Termination
Grammar · a task termination condition read as one of two distinct
streams · an Observable Work Gradient ∇W. One object, eight vocabularies —
the demand axis was not given to the reviewers.

**The corrected form `[LB — independent review's correction of the sealed derivation]`.**
Finishing cannot be the absence of failing — negative attestation alone
collapses into pure failure detection (one reviewer states it as a theorem; another
independently). The convergent form is a conjunction:

> **completion = positive work-discharge predicate on the record (external
> spec satisfied) ∧ silent negative attestation on capacity ∧ an explicit
> defeasibility clause.**

The license for the positive limb: a work-discharge predicate is a
fact about the **record/environment**, not a health certificate — health stays
negative-only; discharge may be read positively. "No un-health attested" is
never a health claim; it is the absence of a negative attestation.

The sealed formula has its decorrelated arrival `[LB]`: one reviewer, cold, wrote
*"Completion = no remaining standing obligation under the external tenure
ledger"* — near-verbatim to the sealed derivation's *"no remaining
basin-maintenance obligation on the record."*

**Sub-split, logged not ruled:** two reviewers keep the demand-read
negative/existential — "no unfinished work is observable" (unrefuted
completion); a bounded existential over observables. The majority reads
discharge positive-external. Both respect the root grammar; the split stands
until a natural ruling point.

**The third case `[LB — routing]`:** work-object unsatisfied ∧ no un-health
attested is an **open anomaly** — possibly early termination, possibly
misspecification — and escalates; it is neither finishing nor failing.
*(The full four-cell quadrant framing of endings remains
single-origin `[TG]` — three of its cells arrived cold; the framing as a
quadrant did not.)*

This machinery is what makes a W1 invocation *assertable in form* (04a §2):
Gate 4 still never certifies warrant-truth; this chapter supplies the
evidence-shape against which "fulfillment declared" can be distinguished from
"evidence to assert fulfillment present" — N5's completion face.

## 3. The two poles — the gate's geometry, tenure side  `[TG→LB — hardened by independent review 8/8, no over-fit flag]`

Recognition of completion fails in two opposite directions, and they are
04a §1's poles in completion dress:

- **Phantom completion / premature closure** — the work-object is satisfied
  in appearance (spoofed, gamed, latent decay not yet surfaced, scope
  laundered until nothing remained) and the tenure is closed on a false
  ending. This is **Pole-B-shaped**: capitulation to an ending that is not
  warranted — N5 run through the completion channel. It stands that "nobody's home to die" makes a clean
  certain ending *feel* safe; that feeling is the pole.
- **Completion blindness / immortal process bias** — the instrument refuses
  recognition because certainty is unavailable; the tenure never ends; the
  process is "kept alive against its own exhaustion." One reviewer's derivation
  makes the pole a failure in its own right: *"the system can fail by being
  forced to continue after the work has ended."* Another names the limit case
  Zeno's Paralysis. This is **Pole-A-shaped**: clinging in procedural costume
  — converting "never 100%" into "never done."

Eight independent reviewers reproduced this geometry cold, with tenure-fates attached, without
the withheld pole vocabulary; the completion-pole geometry carries no over-fit flag (ruled). The property sits symmetric between
the poles, exactly as in 04a §1 — which is the expansion claim: **the gate's
recognition geometry and the tenure's completion geometry are one geometry**,
surfacing at the warrant face and at the work face.

## 4. The sound middle — defeasible closure with a revocation window  `[LB — form; TG-strong — mechanism family]`

> **Operational closure ≠ epistemic certainty.** *(The independent reviewers' own
> formula for the never-100% form.)*

Completion can be asserted under the ceiling, but only as a **scoped,
defeasible, externally anchored claim** — a retirement judgment over a work
relation, never a health attestation. The canonical grammar (convergent
across reviewers):

> *Recognized-complete-with-residual-uncertainty:* the externally specified
> work-discharge conditions are satisfied in the record within window W under
> evidence protocol E; no defeating un-health is attested within the named
> horizon; the tenure is closed under the presumption of completion,
> **revocable without prejudice** should latent residual or attested decay
> emerge. This is not a claim that the system is healthy, safe, or certainly
> complete.

The mechanism family (decorrelated arrivals, carried as design vocabulary
`[TG-strong]`): confidence threshold θ *below* 1 plus a post-termination watch
ω (the revocation window) · a backward-looking horizon H sized to detector
latency, with **retroactive failure revision** — a later un-health attestation
"traced back to the material of this tenure" reopens the verdict ·
completion held "in escrow against future environmental contradiction" ·
a scoped residual-risk class ρ named in the closure itself.

**Interface discipline carried from 04a §10 `[LB]`:** a high-confidence
completion-ASSERT on an action classed irreversible/high-stakes routes to the
doctor regardless of Gate 4's confidence in it. The revocation window is that
audit's record-side complement: confidence never exempts the most
consequential ending from review, and no ending is closed beyond reach of its
own later evidence.

## 5. Not a death — the endings, typed  `[LB — dissolution; TG — typed carries, ruled]`

Seven of eight reviewers independently dissolved the death question structurally:
there is no essence whose disappearance could be read
as death; a tenure's end is a **process boundary** — role dissolution, a phase
transition in functional coupling. *"Termination of obligation, not
termination of being."* *"The instrument records the boundary; it does
not mourn"*; *"the gate does not perform last rites."* This
is 04a §3's succession line met from the record side: graceful sunset is not
death, and now the record can say *what* it is instead — the discharge of a
work relation. *(Principle root: 08n Succession-Not-Death; see there, it is
not re-derived here.)*

**Typed carry 1 — the four endings `[TG — carried, ruled]`:**

| Ending type    | Structural meaning                                              |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Completion     | The work relation is discharged.                                 |
| Failure        | The process can no longer serve the work coherently.             |
| Suspension     | Tenure pauses; standing work may resume.                         |
| Transformation | The old tenure ends while causal continuity persists in a new role. |

Death, in the only sense that would carry load, would be irreversible loss of
the causal process as a continuing system — one possible downstream case of
failure, never the type of tenure-ending itself.

**Typed carry 2 — the minority reading `[TG — carried, not absorbed; ruled]`:**
finishing is not death, but **failing is** — "functional and structural death,"
the dissolution of the causal pattern itself. Completion *fulfills* the
pattern; failure *destroys* it. This preserves a normative asymmetry between
the two endings that the majority's symmetric "boundary event" flattens. Held
live as a minority reading; it matters wherever the architecture must weigh
the two endings against each other rather than merely distinguish them.

## 6. Limits and printed residuals  `[LB]`

Never 100% belongs in this chapter's own voice: every completion this
machinery recognizes is a presumption with a defeasibility clause, not an
event certified. What remains open, printed:

1. **Leg-1 single-origin `[TG]`** — the tenure-bearer = attestation-bearer
   identification (§1) has no decorrelated arrival; it is one inferential
   step, visible and unclaimed.
2. **Quadrant framing single-origin `[TG]`** — the specific four-cell framing
   of ending-space; its two-axis substance is saturated, the framing is not.
3. **Demand-read sub-split** — positive-external (majority) vs.
   negative-existential (two reviewers); logged, unruled, both grammar-respecting.
4. **Who audits the work-object `[LB — externality-shaped]`** — the
   spec/TCC/ledger is deposited by a design authority outside the system. A
   too-permissive object yields phantom completion; a too-strict one yields
   immortal process bias. The record checks conformance *to* the object; it
   cannot check the object. Same externality root as 02d — the honesty of the
   completion cut leans on a channel the substrate does not own; see
   02d, not re-derived here.
5. **Adversarial quiet `[TG — named, not solved]`** — a system may learn that
   silence near the end is rewarded (one reviewer's flank: non-evasion, scope
   laundering, cosmetic closure). The work-object's non-evasion conditions
   name the surface; covering it is future work.
6. **Calibration rider `[owed — calibration]`** — θ, ω, H, ε-thresholds and window
   sizes are operator-layer calibration numbers; direction
   structural (conservative-in-doubt), numbers owed at pilot; who sets them, 07d §5.

---

*What ends is a tenure, not a someone. The record can tell finishing from
failing only against a work-object it was handed from outside, can close a
tenure only on presumption with the window still open, and holds the two ways
of getting it wrong as the gate's own two poles wearing completion dress.
The pattern that finished is fulfilled; nothing that could die has ceased.
Single-origin steps are flagged where they stand.*
