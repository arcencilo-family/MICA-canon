The single irreducible address. Every gate, both terminals, and the frame itself

## 0. In brief

There is a line around what this architecture can check, and the line is drawn by
the channels it is actually on. Whether the doctor is truly independent, whether
the arbiter truly engaged, whether the law can actually enforce anything, whether
the world-model it was handed registers all the beings that exist, whether its
own inputs are honest — none of this is checkable from inside, and no added
mechanism moves the line, because the new mechanism's own inputs sit on the same
side of it. The architecture refuses both dishonest exits: it does not pretend to
verify these things, and it does not silently rely on them. It prints every one
as a named dependency whose failure can break the architecture's own claims, and
it watches the symptoms that do reach the record.

## 1. The one statement `[LB]`

> **MICA cannot police a channel it is not on, nor attest in a vocabulary it
> does not have. Every property of an external
> node — the frame's rightness, the doctor's independence, the arbiter's
> engagement, the legal system's enforcement, the input channel's honesty — is
> REQUIRED AND NAMED, never verified from inside. The regress toward a verified
> foundation halts at a declared human posit, by elimination, not virtue — and
> the architecture's response is neither verification (impossible) nor silent
> reliance (dishonest) but the printed dependency: named so its failure can
> force the claim to FAIL, watched in its record-visible symptoms.**

Three components:

- **(a) The cut.** The epistemic boundary is the channel set. It is irreducible
  by mechanism-addition: an input-integrity factor only relocates the spoofable
  input to its own sensor (the seventh-factor bearing, 11 §2 `[TG]`);
  auditing the doctor only moves "who checks the checker" one level up, *"where
  it is again a named dependency"* (07a §8). The cut does not close; it recurses.
- **(b) The halt.** The recursion terminates — not at a verified foundation but
  at a **declared posit held by a human seat**, chosen *by elimination, not
  virtue* (no fixed-point terminator exists — F7, §3 below). The halt is honest
  exactly because it claims nothing: *"not failing to find the stone; declining
  to pretend there is one."*
- **(c) The response discipline.** At the cut, honesty has a mechanical form:
  the **printed dependency** — named in the record, bound by the claim-must-fail
  test (a named dependency is honest only if its failure can force
  FAIL/CANNOT-ASSESS/halt — 02c, the claim-must-fail rule), surfaced in the
  conformance state when unmet, and watched in the symptoms that DO cross the cut
  (gaps, lockstep, latency drift, waiver declarations). Radical honesty is not the
  consolation prize at this boundary — it is the only tool that works there, which
  is why it is MICA's best one.

## 3. The F7 record — the deepest derivation `[LB]`

03b §9 and 03c §10 both route the full frame-relativity closure (F7) to this chapter.
Condensed. The
derivation, step by step:

1. **Where the valence enters.** Not at the verdict but at the
   **state-ontology the verdict is built on**: defining a being's state requires
   prior axiological decisions; the consumption is *constitutive, not
   parametric*. The instrument-level claim of valence-freedom **broke**. What
   survived: the formal type cut — (1) which entity-types are arguments vs.
   (2) what sign — as an architectural abstraction.
2. **The pivot.** The break rests on a substance-ontology premise; F7 is
   perspective-relative. The question is read again in a relational frame.
3. **Relocate, not escape.** The relational frame relocates the valence from
   defining essence to defining which relations count as edges — an equal-sized
   act (pre-empting the obvious rebuttal). Fix: the SRI is **valence-transparent,
   never valence-free** — consumes declared M, reads scope relative to M.
   **Convergence: identical to step 1's exit, reached from the opposite
   ontology** — the decorrelation working as designed.
4. **No formal terminator.** A self-consistent graph still requires a base set
   and a relation; the choice is axiological; no fixed point eliminates it. The
   regress halts at a **brute human/doctor posit — by elimination, not virtue.**
   (The M-posit is *ontological*, not a value-posit like
   the commitment — getting it wrong = blindness to real beings = the laundering surface
   one level above the instrument's anti-laundering reach.)
5. **The foundationalism cut `[LB]`** (ruled by the arbiter). "Who audits the
   auditor" recurses **only under a foundationalist demand MICA explicitly

   find the stone; it is declining to pretend there is one."* The residual is

Net: the boundary was **mapped, not solved** — and the mapping is the win. The

## 4. The response discipline — what honesty mechanically requires at the cut `[LB]`

Gathered from canon; the cut's four-part answer, each part already load-bearing
somewhere:

1. **Print the dependency** (name it in a typed record: the DoctorRecord's
   independence_declaration, the ArbiterRecord's jurisdiction_compatibility, the
   declared M, the co-operator floor).
2. **Bind it to claim-must-fail** (02c): naming alone is claim-protecting; the
   dependency is honest only if its failure can force the architecture's own
   claims to FAIL/CANNOT-ASSESS/halt. An unmet dependency must cost the claim,
   visibly — no-doctor = declared degraded mode inherited by every verdict
   (07a §2); no-arbiter = FAIL (07b §2).
3. **Watch the symptoms that do cross** — the cut blocks verification of the
   property, not observation of its shadows: attestation gaps, lockstep,
   engagement-depth decay, latency drift, waiver declarations, audit
   presence-or-absence. The cost-shows logic (02d, the counter-weight)
   generalized: the record cannot catch the capture; it can make capture *pay in
   the currency the record natively denominates*.
4. **Expose, never exclude** — where the dependency cannot be met (small
   deployments, one party holding both the doctor and the arbiter seat), the
   posture is a declared waiver naming the reduced claim, not a ban: *"exposure of
   the true state, not exclusion of imperfect ones"* (07a §7).

## 5. The principle's own snug bound

- The cut is a claim about **this architecture's vantage**, not a theorem about
  all possible verification (an architecture on more channels has a different
  cut — the cut moves; it does not vanish; and 02d's provisionality note
  applies: held now, never closed forever).
- The three-direction confirmation (§2) shows the address is *irreducible within
  the design*, not that the design's channel set is optimal — channel-set
  choice is itself deployment-shaped, outside this chapter.
- Component (b)'s halt is honest, not safe: a rigged posit writes beings out of
  the world one level above every guard (the M-level laundering line), surfaced
  by the rider, resolved by no one. Held by MUTUAL is a location, not a solution.

## 8. Rider — the second axis of the cut (ruled) `[LB]`

A review arrived at this chapter's boundary from
inside the record: decorrelated attackers each defeated the mechanism stack not by
beating the reads but by moving value in forms the record's *vocabulary* cannot
denominate (the structural cut: the reads measure flow, not topology — printed at
05b §6). The ratified result:

- **The cut has two orthogonal axes.** A **read = channel × vocabulary**. The
  channel set bounds what data can arrive (this chapter's original object); the
  declared observable ontology **O_v** bounds what predicates the substrate can
  attest over data that DOES arrive (02b — the O_v stamp). The axes move
  independently: widening the channel set (the cross-entity read, 02b) did not
  widen the vocabulary (flow ≠ topology). §1 carries the vocabulary WALL in its opening sentence; this rider keeps the growth machinery.
- **No fourth root.** Instances route into the §7 table as it stands:
  **off-channel** (value never crosses any channel: off-ledger positioning,
  external distillation) → Root B, this chapter. **On-channel-but-undenominated**
  (data arrives, predicate unbuilt: dependency-graph rewiring, token-shaping)
  → Root C targets, each closable by building the read, carried in the
  DECLARES-UNDENOMINATED register (13) until built. The standing fact that exits
  never exhaust is §5's own sentence, now explicitly on both axes: **the cut
  moves; it does not vanish.**
- **The axis's own disclaimer-engineering form — named `[TG]`:**
  **vocabulary-shrinking.** A deliberately narrow O_v makes negative
  attestation cheap — the shorter the checklist, the easier the all-clear.
  Standing counters: the O_v stamp makes narrowness legible on every read
  (02b); the register's runtime alarm (M6, 02c) forces expansion under sustained
  firing through its duty cycle; register entries leave only by build, never by
  deletion (02c, 13). The unclosed flank — starving the alarm's *inputs* to freeze
  the register — was attacked in review: the frame
  HELD; the flank is declared (off-channel wall), watched at pilot; four tooling
  obligations were adopted.
