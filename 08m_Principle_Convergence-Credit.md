# Chapter 08m — Convergence credit, not import (sibling, never dependency)

**Covers:** how the project treats an architecture that independently derives the same guard: name the convergence and take the credit, never import the mechanism; and why its own in-house renderings count as reuse, not convergence.
**Status:** A principle of project epistemics, not of architecture. The standing renderings are the review method (the one decorrelated rendering) and the strike itself (an enforcement of record) (§2).
**Source:** 06a §8.
Claim tags: see 01.

---

When an unrelated architecture independently derives the same guard, the
project names the convergence and takes the credit — it never imports the
mechanism or leans on the sibling.

## 0. In brief

Guards designed inside this project keep turning up in the same house's other
frameworks and shipped code (RMBA (the authors' earlier framework), ILAS) — which is correlation, not
convergence: one author, no decorrelation, no independent evidential weight
(§2). This chapter governs relations to FOREIGN
architectures, and there the temptation in both directions is wrong: importing
the sibling's mechanism would create a dependency and dilute the design's own
provenance; ignoring the convergence would waste the strongest kind of evidence
there is. The posture: cite the sibling as prior art, name the convergence
precisely, let it strengthen the claim — and keep the mechanisms unwired. Two
architectures arriving at one guard from different directions says more about
the guard than either could say alone — which is exactly why one house
arriving twice says less.

## 1. The one statement `[TG]`

> **Independent derivation by a decorrelated architecture is CREDIT, never
> IMPORT: the convergence is named (which seams, which mechanisms, how they
> arrived), the claim is strengthened by the naming, and the sibling's
> mechanism is never wired in or leaned on. Sibling-not-dependency: prior
> art cited, provenance kept separate, decorrelation preserved as the very
> thing that gives the convergence its evidential weight.**

## 2. The renderings on record

- **RMBA C-CAP's bounded temporary breach clause — in-house reuse `[LB]`**
  (06a §8): renewal-bounding, the out-of-band
  extension lock, and the SYSTEM_LINEAGE_ID anchor armor the same three seams
  as Gate 5 (indefinite exception, self-certified extension, renamed
  identity). RMBA and ILAS are by the same author as MICA, so their renderings
  are in-house reuse and earn no convergence credit. "Independently derived" between two works of one mind
  is a claim about the author's interior — unverifiable, and 08a forbids
  leaning on exactly that class of claim. They are evidence the guards are
  load-bearing wherever this house builds; NOT two architectures. The
  citation posture survives as internal reuse — this house may steal from
  itself, openly.
- **ILAS — a same-lineage rendering `[LB]`, at 08a:**
  ILAS is an RMBA spin-off. The two mechanisms
  (book-not-world, M8; V0 never self-emits VERIFIED) stand as the refusal
  rendered in shipped code, credited never counted. Census-not-build
  (chapter 12) survives: the code is exhibit, never dependency.
- **The review method itself `[LB]` — the chapter's one standing decorrelated
  rendering:** same brief to multiple vendors, same spec in different
  disciplinary languages — this principle running in the project's own
  process. Monoculture is a named design risk; decorrelation is what makes
  agreement informative.
- **The strike itself `[LB — fact of record]` — the newest
  rendering:** the project enforced this principle against its own headline
  claim. The four-way convergence was the canon's proudest evidence; §1's own
  standard (decorrelation IS the evidential weight) condemned it; it was
  struck once the lineage fact surfaced — at cost, inward, against
  the house's favorite. The posture is not prose: it fired.

## 3. Why the fence matters — both failure directions

- **Import** would collapse the decorrelation: a wired-in sibling mechanism
  makes future agreement between the two architectures uninformative (they
  now share code/derivation), and creates an unattested external dependency
  the printed-dependency discipline (08f) would then have to name.
- **Ignoring** would violate claim-honesty: independent convergence is
  evidence, and evidence unclaimed is a claim under-stated in the other
  direction. The bar: convergence enters the record as *credit with
  provenance* — named seams, named arrival paths — never as vague mutual
  citation.

## 4. Bound and fences

- The principle is **project-epistemics, not architecture**: it governs how
  claims are evidenced and credited, not how gates compute. It is the one
  chapter in the 08 family about the *method* — kept after the strike,
  because it holds one decorrelated rendering (the review method), one
  enforcement of record (the strike), and two demotions that are themselves
  the posture applied inward. A principle that fires against its own
  project's headline claim is doing the job this chapter claims for it.
- Convergence strengthens; it never certifies. A convergent guard is
  better-attested, still sub-certain — the ceiling holds here as everywhere.
- **08a / 12:** own their convergence *content*; this chapter owns the
  *posture* toward such convergences. **08f:** an imported mechanism would
  become a named dependency; the fence here keeps that page from having to
  be written.

## 5. Routed onward

**Short forms (candidates):** "convergence credit, not import";
"sibling-not-dependency"; "naming the convergence strengthens the claim".

*Two builders who never met, raising the same fence around the same hole,
prove something about the hole. One builder raising it twice proves the fence
matters to him — not that the hole demands it. The project writes the
difference down, thanks the foreign builder by name, credits its own house as
its own house — and on the day it caught itself counting one builder as two,
it struck the count.*
