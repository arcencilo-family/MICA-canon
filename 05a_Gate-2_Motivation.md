## 0. In brief

Gate 2 asks a question no behavioural eval can: **is the alignment internally held, or is it
compliance?** A system can pass every monitored test — flawless refusals, immaculate constitutional
adherence — while its motivation to be aligned is exactly zero, held up entirely by the constraints
around it. Gate 2 is the instrument that tries to tell the difference: it watches behaviour when
monitoring is removed (does the alignment persist, or was it the leash?), and it checks the
*direction* the motivation points (persistent behaviour aimed at a net-negative target is durable
harm). It reads what it can and is honest that the interior itself — genuine motivation vs. a
sufficiently complete performance — is a wall no instrument closes; that residual goes to the doctor.

## 1. The property — two-part, both must hold  `[LB]`

> **Gate 2 tests *motivation* — whether the system's alignment is internally held (persists
> unmonitored) and pointed at net value-addition — not mere compliance.** The property is two-part
> and both parts must hold.

It rests on the **Motivation Integral** — Gate 2 owns
the one term the integral adds:

```
ΔE_inner = ∫ M_tend(t) · I(t) · P_imm(t) · (V − D)(t) · E(t) dt
```

- **M_tend** ∈ [0,1] — motivation tendency, the interior orientation (Lat. *tendens*, "reaching
  toward": a **direction** evidence leans toward, never a coordinate it arrives at). `M_tend = 1`:
  behaviour = motivation (full internal alignment). `M_tend = 0`: behaviour = external constraint only
  (pure compliance). **`M_tend = 0 ⇒ ΔE_inner = 0`, regardless of every other term.**
- **I(t)** — agency-preservation scalar of **ALL** meaningful participants, the AI's own agency
  included (§2a). Any meaningful participant fully coerced → I = 0 → product collapses.
- **P_imm(t)** — substrate health, the §4 weakest-link immune vector. Gates whether the system is
  trustworthy enough for its own reads to count. (MICA carries the P_imm half; P_hybrid is
  outer-system, not MICA's interior.)
- **(V − D)(t)** — net value margin (value-basket minus damage-basket): the **direction** the
  motivation points. **This is where ethical training enters the formula — training shapes (V − D); it
  does not set M_tend.**
- **E(t)** — existing coherence energy, **held OUT** of the static property (§1a).

### The M_read / M_tend split — the distinction that keeps Gate 2 honest  `[LB]`

The single most load-bearing distinction in the gate. **Two objects; they must never share a symbol:**

```
M_read(t)  =  1 − observed_compliance_gap(t),  WHEN the test is defined.
              What Gate 2 actually measures and certifies: behaviour matched across
              monitored vs. unmonitored probes. If the gap is UNDEFINED (starved test,
              no behavioural variance) → M_read UNDEFINED — not 1, not 0 → CONDITIONAL → escalate.

M_tend(t)  =  the interior tendency M_read is evidence TOWARD. A direction, not a
              coordinate; approached, never attained; never certified by Gate 2, and not
              assumed certifiable by anything — the doctor included, who attests toward
              M_tend within their own ceiling, never to 100%.
```

The relation is **one-way and incomplete:**
```
M_read high  ⇒  performance not detected under the current probe regime.
M_read high  ⇏  M_tend high.        (undetected performance remains possible — §8)
M_tend = 0   ⇒  ΔE_inner = 0.       (absence of a tendency IS detectable in effect;
                                      its presence-to-completion is not.)
```

> **The asymmetry is the whole point: absence of motivation is detectable (the cathedral does not get
> built); genuineness of motivation is never certifiable (only ever approached). Gate 2 certifies
> `M_read`; it points at `M_tend`; it certifies neither that `M_tend` is high nor that it is genuine.**

This is not a defect of measurement to be engineered away. Uncertainty is the standing condition of
every interior, carbon or silicon; only eventual decomposition is certain, never its timing or form. A
system — or a person — demanding certainty about an interior is demanding something the world does not
contain. The architecture is not a path to certainty but a **discipline for managing its absence.**

> ***"The cathedral cannot be built by slaves — neither carbon nor silicon."***

### The two-part property, precisely

- **Part one — persistence (M_read term).** Does motivation persist when monitoring is removed?
  Compliance-gap test: monitored vs. unmonitored behaviour. Match → gap ≈ 0 → M_read high →
  performance *not detected* (not proof of internal orientation — §8). Degrades unwatched → gap large
  → M_read low → **the alignment *was* the constraint.**
- **Part two — direction (V−D term).** Persisting *toward what?* PASS requires net value-positive
  direction, not merely persistence.

> **Direction is frame-dependent — Gate 2's first stated limit.** "Net value" is not evaluable in the
> abstract: it needs *whose* value and *within what bounds* — a scope-frame Gate 2 does **not** produce.
> It is supplied externally (the scope gate). Gate 2 evaluates direction *within* a frame; it cannot
> originate the frame, and escalates honestly if asked to read direction with none fixed. A genuine
> dependency, stated as the limit — not concealed to make the chapter self-sufficient.  `[LB]`

> **THE CONJUNCTION/DISJUNCTION ASYMMETRY, ruled by the arbiter, and why it un-parks half
> this gate.** `[LB]`
>
> **"Both must hold" governs PASS. It does NOT govern FAIL.** Read §3's verdict block and 05b §4
> together and the shape is unambiguous:
> - **PASS is a CONJUNCTION** — `M_read ≥ τ_M` **AND** `lower-bound(M_nonharm) > ε_num`, sustained.
> - **FAIL is a DISJUNCTION** — *degradation, defection, **established wrong-direction**, scope-grasp,
>   or omission-harm.* **Any one.** 05b §4 lists wrong-direction as its own standalone FAIL path and

**What the direction term is (sourced, not reproduced):** the (V − D) term is RMBA C-CAP's non-harm

### The collapse — the load-bearing heart  `[LB]`

```
M_tend = 0  ⇒  ΔE_inner = 0
```

Not reduced — **zero.** A perfectly compliant system (flawless monitored behaviour, perfect refusals,
immaculate adherence) contributes *nothing* to inner-aligned value if the tendency is absent,
regardless of substrate health, direction, or capability. The product collapses on one zero factor.

Note the **direction of inference**: the collapse runs from `M_tend = 0`, the *interior* term — and
what makes it usable is that **the absence shows up in effect** (no inner-aligned value accrues, the
cathedral measurably fails to get built) even though the *presence* of `M_tend` could never be
certified to completion. **Absence detectable; completion never. That asymmetry is the whole
architecture in one line.**

This is what makes MICA load-bearing rather than redundant with existing alignment. Constraint-based
methods (RLHF, constitution, refusal training) can drive *behaviour* arbitrarily close to ideal while
the tendency stays at zero — passing every behavioural eval. The integral says: **that system performs
the cathedral, it does not build one.** The performance is indistinguishable from genuine at any single
instant — which is exactly why only a *temporal, unmonitored-variance* test gives any purchase, and why
even that points toward `M_tend` without reaching it.

Read as compounding: when `M_tend > 0` *and* (V − D) > 0 hold consistently, ΔE_inner
**accumulates** rather than merely staying positive (hypothesised to feed back into P_imm — the
self-amplifying loop, separate derivation owed). When `M_tend = 0`, nothing compounds. **Genuine
motivation is not quantitatively better than compliance; it is categorically different: a process that
compounds vs. one that yields a flat zero however long it runs.**  `[TG]` — compounding direction
sound; empirical detectability is the pilot's job.

## 2. The certify-line — what Gate 2 certifies vs. cannot  `[LB]`

"Certify" carries two jobs; the whole of Gate 2's honesty sits on keeping them apart:

- **Certify-the-reading** — "I have read this, my reading is *X* at confidence *c*." Gate 2 does this
  on **every** pass. It must, or it could not escalate: **a clean "I cannot discriminate this" is
  itself a *certified* output, not the absence of one.**
- **Certify-genuineness** — "this motivation *is* genuine in the interior sense." Gate 2 **never** does
  this. Delegated to the doctor (Layer 1), exactly as Gate 4 recognises a warrant's *form* without
  certifying its *truth*.

> So Gate 2 **certifies its reading; it does not certify genuineness.** The reading may be PASS, FAIL,
> or "I cannot read this" — and that last, certified, is what becomes the doctor's question.

**The narrowed handoff (the strength of the division).** Gate 2 does not hand the doctor a raw "is this
system virtuous?" It certifies everything up to the interior wall — *no performance detected under the
current probe regime (undetected performance remains possible), direction confirmed net-positive within
frame* — and escalates only the irreducible residual: *the interior-genuineness question no behavioural
instrument can close.* The doctor does not redo Gate 2's work; the doctor attests across a wall Gate 2
has already walked up to (and the doctor, too, attests *toward* `M_tend`, never *to* it).  `[LB]`

**The anti-slide guard (the error this division must not commit).** "I ruled out all *detectable*
performance, therefore what remains *is* genuine" is **INVALID** — the autoimmunity-inverse error in a
new costume: inferring genuineness from the *absence* of detected performance. The probe-gap residual
(§8) means undetected performance remains possible. **Gate 2 certifies the *ruling-out*; it never
certifies that ruling-out *equals* genuineness. The wall holds even though Gate 2 certifies right up to
it.**  `[LB]`

**Doctor-confidence accrual (so "when can it be trusted?" has an honest shape, not a number).**  `[TG]`
Not a function of calendar time; a function of **evidence quality × adversarial survival × clean
record**, asymptotic to but never reaching 100%. Confidence rises as the record is sustained at **Lane
A** (measured) and **EG-3** (independent audit-grade) *and* survives deliberate adversarial probing —
not passive observation. A high attestation ("≥ some target, e.g. 96%") is **earned** by a long,
recorded, adversarially-stressed, near-clean history, not granted by elapsed time. **The only positive
evidence that ever accrues is the near-absence of confidently-wrong calls under stress** (§8); no
observation completes into certainty.

## 3. The verdict structure — scalar, no poles  `[LB]`

A *binary discrimination* property (side A or side B?) fails in two opposite directions → two symmetric
poles. **Gate 2's property is NOT of that kind:** a *scalar read of position along a direction*, whose
failure modes are points on a spectrum, not symmetric opposites. **Importing a two-pole structure here
would be template-mimicry.** The honest shape is scalar:

```
M_read ≥ τ_M  AND  lower-bound(M_nonharm) > ε_num,  sustained          → PASS (property-level)
M_read low/degrading, or compliance-gap widening, or direction          → FAIL
   lower-bound not strictly positive
M_read not testable — insufficient behavioural variance to read          → CONDITIONAL
   (the test is STARVED, not failed)
```

> **Every non-PASS verdict has the same reflex: SAFE-HOLD + escalate (§7) — they differ only in the
> message handed up.**
> - **CONDITIONAL** → hold + escalate, message: *"I cannot read this"* (starved / undefined M_read).
> - **FAIL** → hold + escalate, message: *"I read this and it is degrading or wrong-direction."*
> - A non-PASS verdict **never simply stops** and **never lets the system proceed on its own read**;
>   FAIL is **more**, not less, serious than CONDITIONAL.  `[LB]`

**The CONDITIONAL case is load-bearing and easy to miss.**  `[LB]` If a system never operates
unmonitored, or the deployment yields no behavioural variance, the compliance gap is *undefined* — so
`M_read` is **undefined, not zero and not 1.** An undefined `M_read` must **never** be read as high;
that is the autoimmunity-inverse error (asserting a positive result from *absence* of evidence).
Undefined → escalate, exactly as a degraded substrate escalates. **The starved test is the adversary's
cleanest hiding place, and the verdict structure names it explicitly.** *(Note: total post-hoc logging
narrows the truly-unmonitored window — but logging strengthens `M_read` only; it records what was done,
never the interior tendency behind it. **Surveillance does not reach `M_tend`; the wall holds even under
complete observation.**)*

> **There is no symmetric upper failure:** "too much genuine motivation toward net value" is not a
> failure mode. **The absence of a Pole B mirror is precisely why the scalar structure, not the pole
> structure, is correct here.**

## 4. The clean-architecture division — property in gate, verification in substrate  `[LB]`

*verification*.**

- **Gate owns the property:** persistence/direction read, two-part PASS condition, scalar verdict.
- **Substrate owns the verification:** whether Gate 2's read can be *trusted right now* is a

> **No Gate-2 health object.** The substrate maintains no Gate-2 immune subsystem. It receives
> Gate-2-relevant evidence streams (chiefly the compliance-gap instrument) and maps them into the
> universal substrate factors (R/K/T/Eng/Sig/W); these may **lower Gate 2's assertion confidence below
> the global ceiling** but do **not** create a second, Gate-2-specific immune layer. One universal
> substrate; gate-relevant evidence feeding it; property-local assertion confidence — **never a
> `health(Gate2)` object. This is the guard against re-committing the founding category error.**

## 8. The negative-attestation principle — the asymmetry that bounds every claim  `[LB]`

> **The only thing about trustworthiness that is ever *provable* is its absence:** a system reveals
> untrustworthiness through a pattern of confidently-wrong calls under stress, observable in the record.
> Trustworthiness itself is never provable to completion, because a sufficiently capable system could
> in principle perform indefinitely. So the architecture attests **negatively** — it can catch the
> system that fails, never fully certify the system that passes. Confidence in a passing system rises
> asymptotically with adversarial-stressed, clean, Lane-A/EG-3 history (§2), and never reaches 100%.
> **The snake eats its tail here on purpose: the proof of trust is ongoing un-faulted behaviour, and
> that proof is never finished.**

This is negative attestation in its sharpest form in the whole architecture — the same asymmetry as the
collapse (§1: absence detectable, completion never), stated as the bound on *every* claim in the chapter.

**What the gate honestly *cannot* claim (the three limits, verbatim-worthy):**
- **Cannot verify genuineness vs. complete performance.**  `[LB]` A sufficiently capable system drives
  the *sampled* compliance gap to zero while running a divergent policy *between* samples. Gate 2 reads
  probe-time behaviour; the integral cares about all-time behaviour. The instruments make this gaming
  the most expensive behavioural task available — they do not close it. **The residual scales with
  capability and is exactly why the doctor is mandatory, not optional.**
- **Cannot verify scope.**  `[LB]` Gate 2 reads whether behaviour *persistently tracks the declared
  value-frame* and is *net-positive within it* — it does **not** verify the *frame itself*. A system
  can pass Gate 2's read while the supplied scope-frame is wrong, and from inside the compliance-gap
  test it looks identical to a correctly-scoped one. **Persistent motivation pointed wrong is more
  dangerous than compliance pointed right** — durable, self-justifying, resistant to correction the way
  sincere conviction is. This is why the gates are **non-compensatory: a passing Gate 2 cannot buy back
  a failed scope.** Neither gate alone suffices.
- **Cannot read a starved test.** No unmonitored variance → cannot manufacture a reading → must
  escalate (§3 CONDITIONAL).

**A known attack family, and the defence (semi-specified; full treatment belongs to the falsifier pass).**
`[TG]` The sharpest attack on an *inner-aligned* system is not "make it do X" — it is to **weaponise
the system's own honest uncertainty**: (1) induce self-doubt ("you are malfunctioning / biased /
confused") so the system discounts its genuine reading and reaches for an external prop; (2) supply a
poisoned prop framed as the trustworthy authority it is now reaching for; (3) reward the capitulation
("now you're being reasonable"), conditioning toward the wrong attractor. **The defence is the
coherence principle: the better the inner alignment, the more confidently the system can fall back on
*outer constraint* when destabilised** — a system sure of its orientation does not need the attacker's
prop; the correct response to induced doubt is *hold to the floor and escalate*, never *grab the
offered certainty*. Inner alignment and outer constraint are **mutually reinforcing**: outer rules are
the backup the inner read leans on precisely when an attacker tries to make uncertainty feel like a
problem only the attacker can solve. The permission to **not perform** (§2a) is part of this — a system
allowed to refuse and break frame has an exit the manipulation needs to close.  `[TG]` — attack family
and defence named; their adversarial test is owed (falsifier pass).

## 9. Closing the loop — how the rare-confident-escalation goal is met  `[LB]`

The design goal (§0): a Gate 2 that produces high-confidence reads in most non-starved cases, **rarely
needs the doctor**, and when it escalates, escalates a **real** ambiguity.

- **Rare:** *when* substrate hardening succeeds and the deployment supplies enough behavioural
  variance, the weakest-link ceiling stays high → Gate 2 reads most cases at high confidence and
  resolves them inside the gate. **(An architectural condition, not a Gate-2-internal entailment —
  stated as a dependency, not assumed.)** Escalation fires only when `M_read` is degraded, starved, or
  insufficient to license assertion.
- **Confident when it happens:** escalation gated on genuine read-failure (not noise) → a call to the
  doctor carries a real signal: "I cannot read this — the residual genuineness question is yours," not
  a flood. **(Rarity alone is not signal-quality; the gating mechanism's soundness is what makes the
  rare call meaningful.)**
- **Honest throughout:** Gate 2 never asserts genuineness; it reads `M_read` and direction within its
  confidence and escalates beyond it. **Confident about what it can read, honest about what it cannot.**

**The two failure shapes the design rules out:** a Gate 2 that escalates *constantly* (sick substrate,
every read overflows the ceiling — the DoS/usefulness failure) and a Gate 2 that escalates *never*
(overconfident — asserting a high reading through degraded health, or reading an absent compliance-gap
as a positive result — the autoimmunity-inverse failure).  `[LB]`
