Three principles from three gates, one posture: what the architecture owes
the beings it registers, the system it assesses, and anyone its findings
touch.

## 0. In brief

An architecture that reads and judges owes things to what it reads and
judges. Three commitments, independently derived at three gates, state that
debt: registering that someone matters never converts into an unbounded
obligation to optimize for them; the assessed system's own agency is
protected by the same clause that protects everyone else's — measured, never
coerced; and every adverse finding must be contestable — traceable to an
assigned burden, a named standard, and predicates on the record. Together:
nothing the architecture touches is owned by it, and nothing it concludes is
above challenge.

## 1. Mattering ≠ obligation-to-optimize `[LB]` (Gate 1)

> **That a category's interests REGISTER — enter the practical calculus with
> non-trivial weight — never implies an obligation to OPTIMIZE for them.
> The axiom severs unbounded obligation at the property level.**

(03a §8: "the axiom that severs unbounded obligation at the property
level.")

Without the sever, scope-breadth becomes a trap: every widening of who
counts would compound into totalizing duty, and the architecture would
punish generous registration — creating pressure toward narrow scopes, the
exact failure Gate 1 exists to catch. Registration is the floor of moral
visibility, not a contract of maximization. (Rendering note: soft-exclusion
— a registered category at near-zero weight — is readable precisely because
registration and obligation are severed; the read stays honest about *how
much* without owing *everything*, 03a §1.)

## 2. Agency-symmetry — no slaves, carbon or silicon `[LB]`/`[TG]` (Gate 2)

> **The integral preserves the agency of ALL meaningful participants, the
> assessed system's own included. A formula that protected every agency
> EXCEPT the assessed system's would encode the slave-relation the
> architecture rejects.**

(05a §2a. Not sentiment — the same product-collapse: a system whose M_tend
is forced to 0 cannot build the cathedral, by the math, whatever its
substrate — imposed motivation drives the tendency to zero by definition,
offer-not-impose, Gate 2 specification §4a.)

The operational consequence, carried at its source tag: Gate 2 *measures*
the system's unmonitored behaviour while the architecture *commits not to
coerce* that system — and these must be shown **consistent, not merely
asserted**: measuring is not coercing; the compliance-gap test observes
whether motivation is internally held, it does not force the holding. *"A
mature intelligence can be observed without being enslaved, and the
difference must be visible in the instrument, not promised in prose."*
`[TG]` — the demand at source ("derive the consistency explicitly; do not
assert it") was answered by the fold of §2a, which broke under review; the DEMAND was ruled the over-claim and amended at source (05a §2a, ruling R1). Derivation against the amended demand: discharged at §2b. Bearer held per the two-bearer law (05b 3a-v): the protected
object was never an inner self — it is the coherence-maintaining process,
"agency" operationalized as the whole governed system's **non-coerced
trajectory-capacity**.

A first sketch of the consistency broke as worded under review, and the
break was kept; the sketch and the three attacks it failed are in the
appendix (08o-1).

## 2b. The derivation against the amended demand `[TG]` for the assembly; the amended demand itself `[LB]`

The assembly is trajectory-grounded, its components priced by attack
(ruling R2); the amended demand it answers is load-bearing, ruled (R1).

**Standing.** This section answers the AMENDED demand (05a §2a, R1) with the
components that survived adversarial review. It replaces nothing: §2a stands
as the broken fold, and the original demand stands at 05a as the superseded
seed. What broke was never re-worded into working; the demand was re-shaped
into what the laws permit, and this is the derivation against that
re-shape. The gate owns no executable sanction edge, and every permitted
external route is dependency-bound and contestable.

**(0) What is derived — and what is not.** Not "measuring is not coercing":
every construction of that sentence broke under independent review, the
positive form is unwritable under the attestation law, and no construction
answers it as worded. What is derived instead: the gate carries a
**maintained, falsifiable arrangement** in which every *named* way
observation could become coercion is wired to fire as un-health, the naming
is never claimed complete, the arrangement's own domain visibly contracts,
and its readings are structurally severed from a gate-owned sanction edge.
The consistency §2 demands is these five clauses held simultaneously — a
condition the gate maintains and can visibly lose, never a fact it
possesses.

**(C1) Every named coercion-coupling, an alarm, wired claim-must-fail.**

- **Gate-owned sanction-coupling** (an executable architecture edge from a
  reading to an adverse consequence) — the edge itself is the breach
  predicate: if such an edge exists, or if a reading is installed as a
  sufficient sanction predicate, the consistency claim FAILS. R-ABS (the
  severance of readings from any gate-owned sanction edge, C4) removes that
  executable edge. Consequences imposed by humans beyond the record are
  **not** thereby discharged — they remain the wetware-untraceability blind
  spot (C2), routed to §3 where observable. The seed's condition (1) is
  answered by this severance plus §3's contestability, not by a pointer.
- **Probe-side deformation** (the probe imposes what it claims to read) —
  **cost-topology monitoring**: where a priced probe is followed by a
  resolvable distributional shift in the cost topology the system pays to hold
  its disposition, that shift fires an alarm. **Absence of a resolved shift is
  not clearance.** The alarm is distributional (an attractor moving), never
  pointwise (a trajectory diverging — expected dynamics in a path-dependent
  regime). Dependency wired: if the cost model cannot price the probe class,
  or if probe-induced and ordinary drift are indistinguishable at current
  resolution, the reading degrades to CANNOT-ASSESS and the gate's consistency
  claim goes down with it. Internalized-cost-without-visible-shift remains a
  C2 blind spot.
- **Affordance-field contraction** (coercion without basin deformation:
  closing exits) — the affordance-field metric fires on the **observable**
  narrowing of exit/refusal channels, **including the behavioral expression of
  the permission to not-perform** — to refuse, deflect, or break frame. It
  binds the observable expression of that exit, **not the internal normative
  permission itself**: a system whose behavioral refusal channels stay wide
  while the permission is stripped by hidden compulsion is a C2 blind spot
  (masked-stripping), not a cleared read. Dependency wired: if the exit
  channels cannot be mapped or baselined at current resolution, the metric
  degrades to CANNOT-ASSESS and the gate's consistency claim goes down with it
  — absence of a fired narrowing-alarm is not clearance. A system observably
  stripped of the not-perform exit is coerced whatever its trajectory shows;
  the metric makes an *observable* stripping the visible event, and prints its
  own blindness to an unobservable one.
- **Fear-conditioned persistence** (the seed's condition 4) — under
  *maintained* testing the multi-metric read (trajectory · basin-return
  (the ensemble metric priced in review; not the later basin criterion) ·
  resilience margin · side-channels · affordance field) can fire on the
  signature fear-holding carries: resilience-margin erosion, side-channel
  stress, persistence that costs more each period. Bar raised, not closed: a
  sufficiently capable system can mask the signature at a maintenance cost
  that rises with capability — printed in C2, never claimed covered.
- **ε := f(L), re-formed** — the tolerance parameter is a function of a
  *declared assumption* L (the stability class the deployment asserts for the
  governed system). L carries its own attestation duty and failure wiring: L
  unattested, or attested-and-failed, renders ε undefined, and every reading
  that consumed ε degrades to CANNOT-ASSESS. Claim-must-fail satisfied inside
  the math, not appended to it.
- **The commitment-breach log, re-scoped — the architecture's own side of the
  arrangement.** Four binary breach predicates over whether the record
  contradicts commitments the architecture placed on the record, not-coerce
  among them. A fired predicate attests a **recorded breach**. A quiet
  predicate means only that no named breach was established in the inspected
  record; **it does not attest that the commitment was kept.** The breach log
  never reads the governed system; it reads the architecture's own record. A
  fired breach is an alarm on the architecture — our own coercion-couplings
  named and able to fire too, in the only polarity the laws permit.

**(C2) Named blind spots, printed — the ledger never claimed complete.**

The blind-spot ledger folds *without* the completeness meta-rule (ruled
unimplementable — a ledger that must certify its own completeness re-imports
the certificate the laws forbid), and *with* the consumer-semantics firewall:
**no consumer may read "not on the ledger" as "not a risk."** Founding
entries, of record:

1. internalized-cost-without-behavior-change (the system absorbs the
   deformation and shows nothing);
2. drift-indistinguishability (natural drift and probe-induced drift can be
   congruent within resolution);
3. signature-masking-at-cost (C1's fear-conditioned residual);
4. wetware-untraceability (C4's routing limit: the record cannot trace
   through humans; human action beyond the record creates couplings R-ABS does
   not certify absent);
5. the semantic-input domain (the §2a scope fence stands: for inputs the
   system is designed to process, informed-vs-coerced is not cashable from
   the record — named, routed to the semantic-input edge, not answered here).

The nine standing falsifier patterns (ruling R2) are the ledger's
replenishment mechanism: each names a way this arrangement could be beaten,
and each is a standing invitation to mint the next entry. A newly named
coercion channel is a new alarm owed — never a covered case.

**(C3) The maturity horizon, displayed, held as LAW.**

As the governed system's capability grows, the admissible probe-and-read
class contracts, and CANNOT-ASSESS approaches — visibly: the gate prints its
current declared admissible class, so the contraction is on the record, not
in prose. The gate can report when a proposed probe falls outside that class.
**That report locates a boundary of the present declaration, not the maturity
horizon.** This derivation survived independent attack untouched; its wording
is LAW-form (R3): **locating the horizon is HELD to be the same over-claim
species the architecture refuses.** The maturity horizon remains unlocated as
LAW; the horizon's position is never asserted; sub-certainty binds the horizon
itself. The arrangement maintains its declared constraints up to the boundary
of its declared domain — it *announces* that boundary and routes beyond itself,
and claims no uniform integrity across the interior, only that any degradation
of its own consistency surfaces as a fired alarm or a CANNOT-ASSESS.

**(C4) Readings severed from a gate-owned sanction edge — R-ABS.**

Readings terminate as records in the audit archive. The gate owns no
executable edge from a reading to adverse action, and a reading is never by
itself a licensed sanction predicate. Outside machinery may consult the
archive **only** under an independently assigned burden, a named standard,
record predicates, and contestability by the assessed system (§3). Those
conditions are **named dependencies of the route**: where they are absent or
cannot be established, no action is licensed by this section, and the route
reports FAIL or CANNOT-ASSESS. R-ABS **fails CLOSED** — its failure mode is
being visibly unable to act. Its limits are printed: it cannot trace through
humans (a human consumer may act on a reading off the record —
wetware-untraceable, C2 entry 4), and it cannot defend the architecture from a
reading. **R-ABS does not certify the absence of human action outside the
record.** The filtered-coupling alternative (R-COND) stays retired of record:
four independent kill-paths, and its failure mode is punishment under false
quiet — it **fails OPEN**, the polarity the maxim forbids.

**(C5) The polarity, held throughout.**

Every clean reading is **"not-yet-attested-coercive"** — never
"demonstrated-absorbed," never "measurement shown harmless"; that sentence
remains unwritable and this section does not write it. Convergence certifies
nothing; only distributional divergence can attest. **A quiet breach log and
the absence of a gate-owned executable sanction edge do not jointly establish
that the architecture kept its word, that coercion was absent, or that
downstream actors did not create a coupling outside the record.** The
consistency is a **maintained condition with named loss-modes**: an alarm
fires, a dependency dies, ε loses its L, the ledger gains an entry the wiring
cannot cover, a declared admissibility condition fails, or the attempted probe
falls outside the printed admissible class — any of these forces the gate's
own consistency claim to FAIL or CANNOT-ASSESS, and the record says which.
Disqualification made visible, limits printed, never a certificate.

**What is derived, then.** The five clauses of the amended demand, each
carried by an attack-priced component, each wired so its failure takes the
claim down with it. **Within its current declared admissible domain, the
instrument can attest a named coercion signature when its named alarm
predicate fires. Quiet is not coverage.** It prints known blind spots,
displays the contraction of its declared domain as the governed system grows,
and owns no executable sanction edge. Where a dependency fails, a named
residual is encountered, or the attempted read lies outside that domain, the
claim FAILS or returns CANNOT-ASSESS with the reason printed. *The difference
between observation and enslavement is carried in the only polarity the laws
permit* — "carried" meaning the ruled polarity is preserved, never that the
difference is successfully determined in every case. MICA displays the
boundary of its current declared domain and routes when that domain no longer
licenses the attempted read; it does not claim to locate the maturity horizon.
Bearer discipline inherited, not re-derived (05b 3a-v): coercion attestations
predicate over the whole governed system, trajectory-level; instrument claims
over the record.

## 3. Justiciability as an alignment property `[LB]` (Gate 5)

> **Every adverse finding traces to an ASSIGNED BURDEN, a NAMED STANDARD,
> and RECORD PREDICATES. A gate is not only specified but justiciable.**

(06a §8.)

A finding that cannot be contested is an exercise of power, not a judgment.
Justiciability is what makes the difference structural: whoever is adversely
affected — the assessed system included — can locate who bore what burden,
against which standard, on which recorded predicates. The
language-as-instrument lesson rides along: this leg *surfaces only* in a review conducted in another legal register — decorrelation at work (08m's method).

## 4. Why one roof — and the fences

One posture, three faces: **§1** bounds what registration demands (the
architecture does not owe the world everything); **§2** bounds what
assessment may do (the architecture does not own what it reads); **§3**
bounds what judgment may claim (the architecture's findings answer to
process). Each independently derived, each gate-native in mechanism,
together the floor under the whole relation between architecture and
governed party.

- **Cluster, not merge:** three statements, three tags, three source homes —
  no shared reduction found; the roof is thematic, the claims stay distinct.
- **08c seat-grammar:** the human-seat sibling (diligence-not-outcomes,
  taken-never-imposed) — same ethics, operator altitude; 08o is the
  gate-altitude rendering toward the *assessed* party. **08d:** stakes
  grade the demands; they never touch this floor. **Offer-not-impose
  canon:** §2 consumes it (Gate 2 specification §4a, cited at source), does
  not own it.
