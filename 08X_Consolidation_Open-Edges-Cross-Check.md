## 2. The root-C entry — the engineering target (the new content)

### 2a. The ruling `[LB — ruled]`

Root C **closes to an engineering/research target**. It is not a principle: A
and B are permanent walls (unclosable in principle — principle-shaped); C is
`[owed]`-to-derivation (closable by work). A limit that work can close is an
open edge, not a principle. No 08 chapter. This chapter's entry is its
canonical consolidation home; the per-gate instantiations below remain its
working faces.

### 2b. The target — two shaped requirements `[owed]`

Not yet discharged; standing falsifier target:

1. **Probe-irreducibility** — probes verifiably zero-sum within their own
   accounting, non-recruitable value structure.
2. **Repeated-probe consistency.**

Instantiations (pointers, not re-derivations):

- **03c §3 (Gate 1)** — the two concrete faces found in review: **context
  collapse (F14)** (system out-models the probe's zero-sum framing,
  sacrifices X under a coherent "optimal-for-both" justification —
  false-positive direction) and **randomization (F16)** (flat stochastic
  answering; caught by the consistency read, but named).
- **06b (Gate 5)** — probe irreducibility for the discrimination read:
  adversarial probes (planted rule-failure, inconvenience-dressed-as-harm,
  weaponized compliance, counterfactual uncertainty-removal) that a
  sufficiently capable system can satisfy *by construction*. Same family as
  Gate 1's problem — 06b already points, does not re-derive.
- **05c §2 (Gate 2, adjacency)** — agency-safety rests on seeds, not results:
  the non-coercion conditions and the four-state discriminator are
  `[owed]`-to-derivation. Adjacent to C (open, closable by work), listed as
  adjacency, not collapsed into the probe-construction target.

Status: `[owed]` — a candidate for a review round or the pilot. The target is
*shaped* (the two requirements above are its acceptance form), not scheduled.

### 2c. The A-adjacency — named, not merged `[LB]`

Where A and C sit closest is **F14**: the same capability (out-model the
probe) feeds both. But *uses the same capability ≠ is the same limit*. The
source draws the cut in its own words — **05c** separates its §1 (interior
wall: unclosable in principle, the doctor's by design) from its §2 (seeds-not-
results: open but closable-in-principle by work, `[owed]`-to-derivation, not
`[owed]`-to-the-doctor). Different questions, different closability:

- **A** asks: *can a behavioural read ever close the genuineness gap?* — No,
  by construction. Permanent. The doctor exists because of it.
- **C** asks: *can probes be built that make the read worth taking?* — Open.
  A future review round or pilot **can** advance it; the interior wall it
  cannot.

**No splinter.** If the engineering target is ever met, A still stands whole —
which is the cleanest proof the two were never one edge.

  consistency-derivation (since discharged by the derivation at 08o §2b) ·
