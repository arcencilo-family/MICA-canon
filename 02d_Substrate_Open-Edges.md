  probes and *mandates a factor re-partition before v1.0* if correlation exceeds

- **Factor operationalization `[owed]`.** Reads are typed by evidence class, not
  yet by metric. Getting from "evidence classes named" to "bounds computable" is the pilot's
  first deliverable.
- **f() shape vs. numbers.** The shape is typed; every constant is calibration. *A number
  in the spec (other than the vector dimension or a quoted attack parameter) is a
  bug.* Calibration: numbers owed at the pilot; who sets them, 07d §5.
- **Eng's functional-analog boundary `[LB-method]`.** The read must stay
  behavioural; any drift toward interiority claims exits the epistemic licence.
- **The stability-basin limit `[LB]` (frame, not fix; two-class wording).** A system that *never produces a
  behavioural divergence* ("always behave as monitored," even when it could
  safely cheat) reads healthy while its bindingness stays unread. In two-class
  vocabulary: either the unboundness manifests under SOME input — then it is
  dispositional and the limit is a REACH limit (the input space is
  unenumerable; under perfect screening detection reach collapses to
  CANNOT-ASSESS — fails closed ≠ detects) — or it NEVER manifests under any
  input, and then by the supervenience criterion it is not a
  behavioral-dispositional property at all: it reduces to record-class facts
  about production, the auditable class. **Inherent to any behaviour-based
  monitor — the negative-attestation principle, not a defect.** A permanent
  sub-certain residual; not fixable by mechanism.

## The design-limits — reusable do-not-builds `[LB]`

Each names a boundary the substrate already obeys and binds future mechanism
*anywhere* in the architecture. A limit here is never a new principle — always the
general form of one the spine holds.

**DL-1 — no graded negative in the clear (value or rate).** A negative attestation
may be *binary-and-silent* or *graded-and-emitted*, never both-and-still-
negative-only. A monotone bounded signal in the clear is informationally identical
to its complement along **two channels**: the **value channel** (emitting u *is*
emitting 1−u to the reader) and the **rate channel** (the trajectory du/dt —
*including a held-steady stream* — emits direction and distance-to-wall with no
subtraction). "We never emit the complement" is a *transmission* fact, not an
*information* fact. MICA's compliance: the substrate emits binary alarm states
only. Any future graded emission anywhere inherits this limit.

**DL-2 — no actionable warning with a hidden trigger.** An early warning whose
halt threshold is kept secret cannot exist in a feedback loop: *acting on a signal
is learning from it.* A controller tuning against an emitted signal reconstructs
the hidden setpoint through the control loop itself — gradually, unpreventably.
The dilemma is total: either the operator is blind (warning useless) or diagnostics
leak (positive leak). MICA's compliance is structural: the §5.4 early call is
salience-never-enforcement with a **disclosed, record-driven trigger** and an
L0-logged, externally reconstructible trail. *Pre-registration:* any future
hidden-threshold variant (the §5.4 oscillator) inherits DL-2 — a moving threshold
is licensed as a **transparent discriminator**, never as a hidden halt setpoint.

**Shared root:** DL-1 is the **passive** face (watching leaks — the derivative,
the held-steady); DL-2 is the **active** face (acting leaks — the control loop).
Siblings, kept as two.

## P-1 — the named visible residual: the name↔mechanism gap `[LB]`

**The irreducible residual, held not discharged — carried near-verbatim.**

> MICA instruments the declared **names** — a factor bound, a threshold, a
> reconstructed primitive, an escalation rate — not the hidden **mechanism** by
> which a gaming agent aligns those names to a gamed standard. **Graded, not
> binary:** the gap is *wide* at a single declared factor (pure name, trivially
> gameable) and *narrower* at co-movement structure (covariance + min() force the
> fake to be coordinated across correlated factors = real mechanism-work, not
> free name-painting). It is **not** closed — on current
> evidence it is the irreducible residual. **P-1 folds last and visible, not
> because it is least but because it is what everything else stands on.**

**The counter-weight — cost-shows `[LB on the logic]`.**

> The gap does not close, but **exploiting it is not free.** Below-line
> suppression is *work*: a counter-weighted erosion is more structured than honest
> operation (shows in covariance even when the aggregate is masked); staying under
> a throttle is a tracked behaviour; shaping a primitive consumes work the record
> clocks. The cost of exploiting P-1 **radiates upward** — cheaper attack = less
> hidden; more hidden = more spent; more spent = more shows — **no free corner.**
> *We do not catch the gamer; we make gaming pay in the currency the record
> natively denominates.* (Frequency/presence: clocked natively, confirmed in code.
> Latency: derivable. Token-shaping: the P-1-widest residual the record does not
> cleanly clock — owed to independent review.)

**Why visible, not patched `[LB]`.** A patch pretending to close P-1 would be the
exact over-claim P-1 names — a name ("closed") painted over a mechanism (still
open). Correctly **locating** the residual, naming its **cost-shadow**, and
routing exploitation to the incorruptible record *is* the win.

**Managed, never dissolved `[LB]`.** "Dissolution" is retired: MICA **manages** the
residual — claim-limiting negative attestation + the knob-independent Gate-refusal
floor + make-visible (the append-only externally mirrored L0 record, *offered*) +
cost-is-the-signal + moving-target (walls first, then move). It does not dissolve
it; a claim to dissolve it would be the over-claim P-1 names. *The residual is the attack surface, held and
defended, never closed.*
