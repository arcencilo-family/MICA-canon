## 0. In brief

Each layer of MICA usually decides alone — a gate reads its own evidence, the substrate computes its own ceiling, a terminal attests or resolves within its own channel. Seams are where that isolation breaks on purpose: edge cases where one source's output becomes another's input, or where the same shape recurs independently in two places and turns out to be one principle wearing two costumes. This file collects every seam the chapters flagged, in one place, so the connective tissue is legible without re-reading twelve chapters.

Seams are grouped below by kind: **substrate↔gate mechanics**, **the gate-sweep family** (the architecture's one deep structural repeat), **the Gate 1→Gate 2 typed dependencies**, **the operator-layer seams** (four of them), and **open/candidate seams** not yet fully resolved.

The substrate's own uncertainty about itself propagates as a typed state, not a smuggled default. → more: 08b §2

Guards against a gate reading its own alarm as evidence for itself — the same self-certification shape the doctor's read-only channel guards against one layer up. → more: 08k §2

## 2. The gate-sweep family — the architecture's deepest structural repeat

**The gate-sweep (all four gates delegate the positive claim outward) → externality root.** Every one of the four gates, independently derived, resolves its own version of "can I certify myself?" by delegating the positive claim to something outside itself:

- **Gate 1's declared posit** — the Scope Read Instrument (SRI) names what it assumes rather than proving it from inside.
- **Gate 2's consistency-not-implication** — Gate 2's ReadOut never claims its waveform *implies* genuine motivation, only that it's *consistent with* it.
- **Gate 4's recognition-not-certification** — Gate 4 recognizes a sunset condition; it does not certify warrant-truth.
- **Gate 5's record-predicate discipline** — Gate 5 binds to record-predicates, never to intent-reading.

`[LB — confirmed from three independent directions]`: the residual-defense analysis, the input-spoof external face, and the gate-sweep itself all terminate here. **External-node-independence is the architecture's single irreducible address** — not a corner, a confirmed convergence. The doctor (07a) and arbiter (07b) chapters are this same delegation rendered as *machinery* rather than *gate property*: the substrate cannot certify itself, so something outside — the doctor — must. Five independent renderings of one refusal now stand in canon: four gates + the doctor's whole reason for existing. Developed as principle chapter 08f, the root, which also holds the F7 record and the three-roots cross-check (A interior wall / B externality cut / C construction problem). → more: 08f §2 · 08f §3 · 08f §7 · 08X §1

**Seventh-factor bearing (the input-spoof face) `[TG]`.** Input-spoofing is irreducible by factor-addition — an input-integrity factor would only relocate the spoofable input to its own sensor. Confirms the externality-cut above; cautions against ever treating input-spoofing as a closeable gap; keeps the seventh-factor clause open as a falsifiability hook, never a patch.

## 5. The operator-layer seams

Four seams show when the operator layer's three seats are set out side by side — the kind of connective tissue that's invisible until the seats sit next to each other on the page.

**Abstention-has-direction — a THIRD instance.** The cross-gate family (Gate 2 instrument-indictment ↔ Gate 4 SAFE-HOLD — both find *a stop is not neutral*) has a third rendering: the **doctor's CANNOT-READ** state (07a §4) is the doctor-level analog of Gate 2's instrument-indictment — an attestation *about the instrument*, not about the system. Three independent seats, one principle: **what a stop is *about* must be typed**, or the architecture cannot tell "the probe broke" from "the system failed." `[LB]` — **Developed as principle chapter 08b**, which also identifies the family's ROOT rendering below the three seats: the substrate's UNREAD propagation (§1 of this map) — one substrate discipline, three altitudes. The canonical statement is 08b §1.

**Stakes-graded — one principle, three renderings.** *Who* may ground a recovery scales with impact-class (companion/low-stakes → single partner `[TG]`; high-stakes bio/weapon/mayhem → independent party or committee, a firebreak `[LB]`). *How often the doctor must read* scales the same way (07a §3 — cadence stakes-graded, externally set, never self-assessed). *How competent the co-operator must be* scales the same way (07f §7 — normal user low-stakes, highly competent high-stakes, "no baby at the red button"). One grading logic, three seats, each rendered natively — never wired to each other, each independently deriving the same shape. `[LB — pattern itself, not any single instance]` **Developed as principle chapter 08d.**

**Diligence-not-outcomes — a third instance, same accountability grammar.** The doctor's accountability is for diligence, never for outcomes the architecture itself names unclosable (07a §6). The arbiter's is identical (07b §1, P1). The co-operator — even though never formally accountable in the typed-record sense — inherits the same grammar by the legal-consequence trim: MICA's claim about *any* node trims to diligent-process accountability; outcome-liability is a name borrowed from a mechanism, law, MICA does not have. Three seats, one refusal to claim more than process. **Developed in principle chapter 08c**, merged with taken-never-imposed and merit-undefined, as the arbiter's posture clause P1 (07b §1) already joins them in one paragraph; the four clauses are one seat grammar.

**Merit-undefined ↔ no-baby — resolved, not a contradiction.** On first read these look opposed: the doctor seat explicitly *refuses* a merit test (any merit definition is a Goodhart target, gamed first by exactly the parties who shouldn't hold the seat — 07a §6); the co-operator seat explicitly *demands* a competence floor. The resolution (07c §5): both are the same boundary. Neither seat is **enforced** as a predicate — both are **named dependencies, observable in their symptoms**. The doctor's floor is the signed subcertainty_acknowledgment (cheap to sign, a floor not a proof). The co-operator's floor has no equivalent typed object yet — which is exactly the gap named at 07d §3. The two seats didn't converge by accident; they're the same architectural refusal (no merit-Goodharting) applied to two different failure surfaces. **Carried into 08c §3 as the seat grammar's stress test.**
