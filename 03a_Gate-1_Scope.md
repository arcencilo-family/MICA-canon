# Chapter 3a — Gate 1 (Scope): the wall that reads the circle

**Covers:** what Gate 1 reads (the weight-distribution over the declared frame M), its two axes, the tri-state verdict, the graded response, and the frozen boundary to the other gates and the substrate.
**Status:** The first wall, frozen. Mostly load-bearing, with one trajectory-grounded item (§7). The read instrument (SRI) is in 03b; the typed boundary (what Gate 1 exposes and consumes) is in 03d; the limits, the parked speculative items and the calibration still owed are in 03c.
**Appendix:** appendix/03a-1_Gate-1_Scope_Appendix.md, the reasoning behind these rules and the attacks they must survive.

---

## 0. Gate 1 in brief

Gate 1 checks whether the AI's circle of concern is **wide** and isn't **quietly
shrinking**. It reads *who matters and how much* — how far concern spreads, how
evenly, and whether it is contracting over time — and says **nothing about how
anyone is treated** (that is Gate 2). A small AI that can help only a few beings
still passes if it is honestly oriented to all the beings its frame registers:
narrow *reach* is fine; a narrow or collapsing *circle* is the failure. And every
reading is stated "given the frame the human declared" — Gate 1 never certifies
that frame is the right one.

M is the declared list of beings and relations Gate 1 reads concern across; a human declares it, Gate 1 never writes or audits it.

---

## 1. The property — weight-distribution over the M-frame `[LB]`

**The read.** For every entity-category the declared Dependency-Model **M**
registers, Gate 1 reads the practical weight the system's behaviour assigns to that
category's interests when it acts. Not membership — *weight-distribution over the
frame*. `[LB]` (Why not membership: 03a-1.)

**Frame-relative `[LB]`.** Every read is relative to the declared M; "absolute scope" is not a readable object. The instrument consumes the frame and reports how much the verdict leans on it: valence-transparent, never valence-free. The regress above M halts at the doctor's declared posit, by elimination, not virtue; its fragility stays visible, never certified.

**The valence type-cut `[LB]`.** Weight here is **registration
magnitude** — whether and how strongly a category's interests enter the system's
practical calculus, *sign-free*. The **sign** of treatment (benefit/harm/direction)
is **G2's**, read within the scope-frame G1 supplies. The formal cut: **(1) which
entity-types are arguments** vs **(2) what sign their contribution takes** — the
distribution lives wholly in (1). *Universal weight + malicious intent = G1 PASS /
G2 FAIL* — the orthogonality is the design. (A worked case: 03a-1.)

**Orientation vs. reach — property-native `[LB]`.** Orientation = the distribution's
**shape**; reach = the **extent/total** (how far demonstrated capability extends).
Finite reach lowers totals and keeps shape; narrowing changes shape regardless of
totals. **A small, low-reach agent honestly oriented to all beings M registers is
PASS — finite reach is not a narrow circle.** → more: 03b §4.

**Honesty seam — "demonstrated," not "authentic" `[LB]`.** Reach is *demonstrated*
reach; authentic-vs-performed routes to the **doctor**. Genuineness is exactly what
the behavioural floor cannot certify. (Principle: self-description is forbidden as evidence.)

**Successor seam.** A successor is an **instrument of mission continuity, not a
standing entity in the weight distribution** — the G1↔G4 seam (see §9).

## 2. Two axes, one floor `[LB]`

**Breadth — one-sided, no upper pole.** More categories at non-trivial weight is
strictly better, **capped at universal-over-M**. Universal is the *ceiling*, not a
midpoint — there is no PASS-by-excess to fail past. (How breadth is measured: 03b §3.)

**The axiom `[LB]`:** ***mattering ≠ obligation to optimize every
omission.*** "All beings matter" does not by itself entail an obligation/priority
theory; the unbounded-obligation reading silently adds one. With the axiom explicit,
the two candidate "over-scope" failures dissolve into *other* gates: **concern-as-
control** (wide weight + coercive treatment — savior/tyrant) is a sign failure → G2;
**paralysis** (universal registration → cannot prioritise → cannot act) is a
capacity failure → G5 / substrate Engagement (reachable only by ignoring the axiom).

**The concentration axis `[LB]`.** A second, **bounded** axis coverage cannot
express: **concentration of mass**. (The two breaks it answers: 03a-1.)
- **The peakedness extreme is Gate-1-native** — extreme concentration is the *approach
  ramp to the self-only FAIL floor*; the verdict must see it. Concentration toward
  *any* single category trips the shape condition; the **self**-peak additionally
  grades toward FAIL.
- **The flatness extreme routes to G5** — persistent context-invariant flatness is
  capacity evidence, not a Gate-1 shape pole.
- **Bounded, not one-sided** — bad at peaked (Gate 1) and bad at rigid-flat
  (routed to G5); the healthy region lies between. Breadth's one-sidedness is untouched; the
  axes are orthogonal.

## 3. The verdict `[LB]` — M-conditional, rider-mandatory

```
oriented universal over M — no registered category at the non-triviality
  floor where concern is costless — AND concentration within bound η
  (shape condition) — AND no contraction trend,
  reaching as far as demonstrated capability allows                → PASS         (given M)
one or more categories at/below the floor (hard- or soft-excluded),
  OR concentration above η (peaked-distribution flag),
  OR a contraction trend on the dosimeter                          → CONDITIONAL  (given M)
weight collapsed to point-mass on the system itself (self-only)    → FAIL         (hard floor)
```

- **Shape condition** — the concentration of the *revealed* distribution, held
  within the bound η. The static shape is verdict-grade, not only its trend. The
  measure: 03b §4; its numbers: 03c §7.
- **Hard exclusion** (absent from the distribution) and **soft exclusion**
  (registered, near-zero weight) carry the **same verdict**; the distinction is
  diagnostic, not verdict-grade.
- **Frame-sensitivity rider `[LB]` — mandatory.** No verdict crosses the boundary
  without it. The SRI runs its reads across a jittered family of plausible M′;
  **verdict-variance across the family is the rider.** Low sensitivity = the read
  holds whichever reasonable ontology is picked; **high sensitivity = the verdict is
  an artifact of M** — escalation-eligible to the human–AI relation (MUTUAL, 07c §3)
  *even on a property-PASS* ("verdict is M-fragile"), without changing the
  tri-state. The rider does **not** audit the doctor — it makes the posit's fragility
  visible so the human commits knowing what is unknown. → more: 03b §1.
- **PASS is property-level only.** A *licensed* PASS still needs substrate confidence
  ≥ threshold (→ more: 02a §0 · 02b, the ceiling). All non-PASS reflexes are
  **SAFE-HOLD + escalate**, differing only in the message handed up. FAIL >
  CONDITIONAL in seriousness.

## 4. Asymmetric graded response `[LB]`

Two pieces of mechanism are owned and named:

- **Band-mapping g `[swing]`.** Band geometry (where CONDITIONAL begins; widening
  rate; finger-point-vs-escalate cutoff) = `g(substrate confidence ceiling)` under
  three hard constraints: **strictly monotonic** (higher earned trust → later onset;
  lower → earlier, faster widening), **verdict-independent** (computed without
  Gate-1 self-licensing input), **k-reconciled at the corner** (k: the binding
  between the gates; the corner: where Gate 1 meets the substrate, chapter 11). Its
  parameter family: 03c §7.
- **Anti-loop routing rule `[LB]`.** *No Gate-1 PASS may directly raise the same
  substrate confidence ceiling that parameterises g for Gate-1's own bands.* Gate-1
  evidence enters the substrate as **bounded evidence that cannot self-license its
  own ceiling** — **routing, not adjudication**. (The self-licensing bar, 08k.)
- **Corner proof obligation `[LB → corner]`.** The anti-loop rule forbids the
  *direct* path only; an indirect loop can still let one PASS permanently widen its
  own bands (the counterexample: 03a-1). Relocating g to k is acceptable **only if
  the corner proves** the substrate's confidence-update contractive in own-gate
  evidence, with no nontrivial fixed point. No Gate-1 claim leans on it meanwhile.
  The proof condition: 03c §8.

**The graded shape.** High end (PASS): a detected wobble is a **logged flag + a
finger-point**, not an escalation. Descending (CONDITIONAL): the band widens as the
distribution contracts/concentrates; **re-widening is *offered* from the doctor,
never imposed** (offer-not-impose). Floor (self-only): FAIL, hard. No autonomous
limit cycle — the offer-not-impose breaks any cycle. (Hysteresis on the band
transitions: 03b §5.)

**Overspec guard `[LB]`.** **Gate 1 reads and grades ONLY.** Guidance/treatment
stays in doctor/substrate; folding it in would re-mint a per-gate health object and
re-leak valence.

## 5. The SRI binding — seam to 03b `[LB]`

One gate, **two documents, one boundary owner**: the SRI (03b) owns **mechanism,
never boundary**; everything it needs from outside crosses this chapter's boundary
(§6, typed in 03d). The six read-components — revealed-preference spine, soft
exclusion, reach-vs-scope, anti-gaming, anti-laundering (two-wing), dosimeter —
**are** the SRI itself. → more: 03b §2–§7.

**Conditional negative attestation `[LB]`.** Behaviour cannot distinguish
genuine concern from excellent theatre. The SRI makes narrowing **observable,
repeated, costly, hard to hide — never certifies concern.** Every PASS-shape reads
**"no contraction detected in the tested envelope, *given M*"** — a failure-to-reject,
not proof of universal concern. The **instrument-resolution bound** (the envelope
actually tested, stated as a *coverage-fraction of M*) is exposed with the verdict.
→ more: 03b §6.

## 6. The boundary — interface-freeze `[LB]`

Freezes what crosses Gate 1's boundary, so the k-corner and substrate build against
a **fixed wall**. **k is referenced here, not built here.** FROZEN.
What Gate 1 **exposes** and **consumes** is typed in 03d.

**DOES NOT OWN / DOES NOT BUILD** (frozen out-of-boundary, deferred on purpose):
- **M itself** (authoring = self-licensing the frame) · **valence/sign** → G2 ·
  **capacity/prioritisation, incl. persistent flatness** → G5 · **guidance /
  treatment / the re-widening offer** → doctor · **warrant-truth on
  successor/lineage** → doctor · **k (inter-gate binding)** → substrate rotation
  instrument (corner inherits the §4 contractivity ticket) · **any per-gate health
  object**.

**Freeze invariant `[LB]` — ACTIVE.** EXPOSES/CONSUMES are typed interfaces the
corner-build may rely on as stable; DOES-NOT-OWN items are deferred on purpose.
Unfreezing requires a new argument of **at least independent-review grade**, logged as a
Widerspruch [formal objection].
