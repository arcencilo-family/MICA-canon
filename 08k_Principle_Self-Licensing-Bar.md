# Chapter 08k — The self-licensing bar (routing, not adjudication)

**Covers:** why no gate may raise its own ceiling with its own evidence, how this differs from negative attestation, and the proof obligation one layer down.
**Status:** A principle at the seam between Gate 1 and the substrate, the anti-loop discipline.
**Source:** 03a §4.
Claim tags: see 01.

---

No gate may raise its own ceiling with its own evidence. The gate routes what
it sees into the substrate; the substrate adjudicates what it licenses.

## 0. In brief

A gate produces evidence about the system — and some of that evidence, if fed
back naively, would bear on how much the gate itself should be trusted. The
hazard is a closed loop: a gate reading its own output as grounds for its own
authority, quietly licensing itself upward. The bar: a gate's own evidence
enters the substrate as bounded input that cannot set the ceiling under which
that same gate asserts. The gate routes; only the substrate — with its
anti-feedback proof obligations — adjudicates.

## 1. The one statement `[LB]`

> **Own-gate evidence enters the substrate as bounded evidence that cannot
> self-license its own ceiling — routing, not adjudication. The gate never
> grades its own trust; the closed loop from a gate's output to that gate's
> assertion-authority is structurally barred.**

(03a §4; the bar's fence is declared at its source, 03a §8.)

## 2. The fence the source itself drew `[LB]`

The list of the principles Gate 1 feeds (03a §8) names the two apart:
*"Negative attestation — 08a"* and *"The self-licensing bar — 08k"*; the bar
is about the closed-loop hazard. The distinction is real, not rhyme:

- **08a** bounds *what may be said* — no positive self-attestation of health.
- **08k** bars *a specific circuit* — the loop from own-output to
  own-authority, even where every individual statement is negatively-framed
  and honest. A gate could obey 08a perfectly and still self-license through
  accumulation; the bar closes that path structurally, not rhetorically.

The mechanical guard one layer down is **the stratification rule (R8,
11 §1)**: the substrate's confidence-update must be proven contractive in
own-gate evidence and over indirect pattern-read paths before any re-coupling
of a gate's own escalations into that gate's own confidence cap. 08k is the
principle; R8 is its proof obligation.

## 3. Bound and fences

- The bar does not silence own-gate evidence — it *bounds* it. The evidence
  is real input (routed); what it cannot do is adjudicate its producer's
  ceiling. Bounded-in, never self-licensing.
- **08g:** different object — 08g refuses a lever over *external parties*;
  08k bars an *internal* circuit. Both refuse a power, at different seams.
- **08h:** the ceiling being protected is 08h's confidence axis; 08k guards
  the axis's integrity against one specific capture route.
- Sibling shape one layer up, noted not merged: the doctor's read-only
  channel guards the same self-certification shape at the substrate/doctor
  seam (11 §1 says this of R8 explicitly).

## 4. Routed onward

- R8 contractivity proof — banked for Gate 1 (corner contractivity, 03a §9);
  standing obligation before any re-coupling elsewhere.
- **Short forms (candidates):** "bounded evidence that cannot
  self-license its own ceiling"; "routing, not adjudication".

*The gate may speak about itself; it may never sit on its own case. Evidence
flows up, authority flows down, and the loop that would let a reader grade
its own trust is cut before it closes.*
