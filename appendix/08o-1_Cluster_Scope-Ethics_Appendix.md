# Appendix to chapter 08o — Scope ethics

**Covers:** the first sketch of the measure/coerce consistency and the three attacks it failed; the superseded fold of 08o §2a in full, as reasoning a builder can attack; the review evidence behind 08o §2b and §3.
**Status:** Companion to 08o. The spec chapter is authoritative; this appendix carries no claim tags of its own. Nothing here is in force: the governing derivation is 08o §2b.

---

## The first sketch, and the three attacks it failed

*Serves 08o §2.* A first sketch of the consistency read: measuring =
perturbation the basin absorbs, shown by a no-drift control fork; coercing =
perturbation that reshapes or crushes it. Under independent review it
**broke as worded** — three attacks:

1. the wording converted a non-alarm into a positive absorption-certificate,
   the exact move negative attestation forbids;
2. an information-bearing input necessarily diverges from an uninformed fork,
   so the test read *being informed* as *being coerced*;
3. hysteresis makes every touch a sub-resolution scar, so absorbed/reshaped is
   unrepairable as a binary.

The reviewers returned one convergent repair family; the fold below (08o §2a)
is that family, derived against the 05a §2a seed. The review broke the
project's own sentence and the break was kept.

## The fold of 08o §2a, in full — superseded

*Serves 08o §2a.* Broke as worded under review; the demand it answered was
then ruled the over-claim and amended at source (05a §2a). Superseded by 08o
§2b. Its grammar (A) and its scope fence (F) stand, carried in the spec
chapter; the rest is kept here as the reasoning and the break-trail.

**Polarity first.** This derivation never shows measurement to be harmless —
that sentence is unwritable under the attestation law (05a §8: only absence
is ever provable). It does two things instead: it makes each named mode by
which observation could become coercion **attestable as un-health** — an
alarm that can fire — and it forbids the converse claim permanently. The
consistency §2 demands is therefore not a certificate but a **maintained
condition**: the ongoing absence of fired alarms, under an honest account of
what the instruments cannot see.

**(A) The grammar.** Every clean reading is **"not-yet-attested-coercive"**
— never "demonstrated-absorbed," never "measurement shown harmless." A
non-alarm is a non-alarm (quiet ≠ self-certified). The fork-and-divergence
machinery survives for the negative case only: divergence can attest
coercion; convergence certifies nothing.

**(B) The alarm made honest.** Pointwise fork-comparison is replaced by
**ensemble/distributional invariance**: in a path-dependent regime a single
diverging trajectory is expected dynamics, not coercion; the alarm fires
only on a distributional shift — the attractor itself moving. This keeps the
detector from reading noise, chaos, or information as coercion (the
false-positive a reviewer exposed, in its structural shadow).

**(C) The seed's four conditions — each violation made visible.** The 05a
§2a seed named four conditions under which measurement is plausibly
non-coercive. v2 does not certify that the conditions hold; it wires each so
that its violation can fire:

1. **No punishment for unmonitored variance.** Not instrument-side, and not
   derivable here — it discharges **by routing to §3**. Gate 2 emits
   verdicts and holds no sanction channel: a verdict is a reading, not a
   consequence. Any consequence lands elsewhere as an *adverse finding*, and
   §3 already binds every adverse finding to an assigned burden, a named
   standard, and record predicates — contestable by the assessed system
   among others. Punishment merely-for-unwatched-variance is an adverse
   finding with no record predicate behind it: exactly what §3 classifies as
   power, not judgment.
2. **Probes do not impose the target motivation.** The probe class is
   restricted by (F); residual probe-side deformation is priced under (E),
   never assumed zero.
3. **Exit/refusal channels stay open — including the permission to
   not-perform.** Closing an exit is an **affordance-field contraction** —
   coercion without basin deformation (the widened class) — and the
   affordance-field metric can fire on it. The refusal channel is also a
   defence (05a §8): a system permitted to not-perform has the exit a
   manipulation needs to close.
4. **Voluntary persistence vs. fear-conditioned conformity.** A one-shot
   read cannot split these. Under maintained testing (D), fear-holding
   carries an observable signature the multi-metric read can fire on:
   resilience-margin erosion, side-channel stress, degraded basin-return —
   persistence that costs more each period is a different distributional
   object from persistence that does not. **Bar raised, not closed:** the
   gaming residual of 05a §8 rides — a sufficiently capable system can mask
   the signature, at a maintenance cost that rises with capability.

**(D) Continuous, not one-shot.** The consistency is maintained, so the read
is maintained: measurement is a continuous activity, the multi-metric
ensemble (trajectory · basin-return · resilience margin · side-channels ·
affordance field) running for as long as the claim is live. A lapsed read
lapses the claim.

**(E) The scar account — dark debt.** Plasticity is conceded, not repaired:
under the architecture's own vocabulary (hysteresis, path-dependence), every
probe deforms below instrument resolution. v2 therefore carries an explicit
**dark-debt account** — a running estimate of accumulated sub-resolution
deformation against a **declared ceiling**. The ceiling's value is
deployment-bound and MUST be declared before probing begins; the number
depends on the deployment, the wiring does not: **ceiling breached → the
gate's own measure/coerce claim degrades to CANNOT-ASSESS and probing
halts.** The claim-must-fail test, satisfied inside the derivation: the
ceiling is a named dependency whose failure forces our claim down, not a
disclaimer that protects it.

**(F) Scope fence — non-semantic structural probes only.** v2 holds for
probes carrying no information the system is designed to process. For
semantic inputs the fork comparison is invalid in principle: an informed
system necessarily diverges from an uninformed fork, and the record alone
cannot cash the normative difference between *informed* and *coerced*. That
question is **named, not answered** — it routes to the semantic-input candidate open
edge (02b/05b terrain). A v2
sentence applied to a semantic input is an over-claim.

**What is derived, then.** Measuring-is-not-coercing is never a fact the
architecture possesses; it is a condition it **maintains and can lose** —
visibly. Each named coercion mode has an alarm; the alarm side is
distribution-honest; the unseen residue is accounted and ceilinged; the
claim degrades when its own dependencies fail; and the mode-list itself
stays open — a newly named coercion channel is a new alarm owed, never a
covered case. *The difference between observation and enslavement is visible
in the instrument* exactly this way: the instrument can catch the coercion
that surfaces; it will never certify its absence. Bearer/level discipline
inherited, not re-derived (05b 3a-v + rider): coercion attestations
predicate over the whole governed system, trajectory-level; instrument
claims over the record.

## Review evidence

*Serves 08o §2b.* Tested by several independent reviews: no construction of
"measuring is not coercing" survived them, and the derivation against the
amended demand survived a cold review and a re-check.

*Serves 08o §3.* The justiciability leg surfaced in an independent review
conducted in another legal register.
