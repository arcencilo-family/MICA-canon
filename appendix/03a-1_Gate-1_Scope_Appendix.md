# Appendix to chapter 3a — Gate 1 (Scope)

**Covers:** the reasoning behind the rules of 03a and the attacks they must survive, for a developer who builds or attacks a MICA machine.
**Status:** Companion to 03a. The spec chapter is authoritative: where this appendix restates a rule, 03a's wording and claim tag govern. This appendix carries no claim tags of its own.

---

## The read — why weight, not membership

*Serves 03a §1, The read.*

A binary set-membership read has a verdict surface blind to soft exclusion: a
category included in name at near-zero weight passes clean. The instrument-native distribution is forced by that mismatch between instrument and reality, not by taste, and it makes room for the concentration axis (03a §2), which membership cannot express.

Tested by a review panel.

## Frame-relative — why the frame is consumed, never chosen

*Serves 03a §1, Frame-relative.*

Choosing what counts as a being's state, or which relations count as edges, is an
equal-sized axiological act either way. The instrument is therefore
valence-transparent, never valence-free: it consumes the frame, reads scope relative
to M, and reports how much the verdict leans on M.

Tested by a review panel, from opposite ontologies.

## The valence type-cut — a worked case

*Serves 03a §1, The valence type-cut.*

A system that registers future beings solely to apply heavy negative weighting
still registers non-zero weight: it is G1-eligible, and it is exactly the case
Gate 2's sign-read exists to fail.

## The concentration axis — the two breaks it answers

*Serves 03a §2, The concentration axis.*

Coverage alone is a lossy projection. Two cases read PASS on coverage and are
not healthy:
- **Peaked-universal:** every category just above the floor, ~99% of the mass on
  the system itself.
- **Frame-invariant skew:** 0.1 : 0.45 : 0.45, held across the whole M′-family.

Tested by a review panel.

## The corner — the max() counterexample

*Serves 03a §4, Corner proof obligation.*

The anti-loop rule forbids only the direct path. With an aggregation such as
`C = max(C, evidence)`, a single PASS can permanently widen its own bands through
the indirect loop. That is why the corner must prove contractivity and the absence
of a nontrivial fixed point on a concrete aggregation rule.
