## The M_read / M_tend split — the collapse it must survive

*Serves 08q §2.* An earlier version of the Gate 2 specification wrote
the measured quantity and the interior tendency as one object. A logic-check
by two independent reviewers caught the collapse. The split exists so that a
compliance measurement can never quietly become a claim about genuine
motivation.
