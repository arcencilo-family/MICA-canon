This chapter covers what Gate 5 cannot do, the numbers not yet set, and the parts owed but not built — for the gate (06a) and its owed instrument.

## 0. In brief

These are the things Gate 5 **cannot** do, the numbers **not yet set**, and the parts
**owed but not built** — kept on the page on purpose. Gate 5 can make a self-granted
exception observable, bounded, and costly to fake; it can **never certify that any given
override was right**, nor that a patient adversary cannot eventually walk a gamed exception
past the declared probes. It reads *behaviour against a record*, never *sincerity* — so a
system that games the record perfectly, or a rule-worship that produces the same null action
as honest caution, both sit at the edge of what the gate can see. None of this is hidden;
naming it is the point.

## The negative-attestation & externality roots — pointer, not re-derived `[LB-direction]`

Gate 5's two deepest limits are **legs of the single root already collected in 02d** — not
separate Gate 5 findings, and not re-solved here.

**Leg 1 — negative attestation (the Ouroboros).** The gate attests the **absence of detected
pole-signatures under declared probes — never soundness**. The general form: negative attestation is fragile without a positive baseline;
a sufficiently weak or clever probe-set is indistinguishable from a clean system. This is **framework-wide — the Ouroboros, already load-bearing across all gates —
not a Gate-5-local defect**. Same root as every other
gate's behaviour-vs-theatre ceiling.

**Leg 2 — the externality cut.** The record-predicate extension rule (06a §4) checks that
raw-state **access** occurred before the decision — it cannot check **engagement**. **Independence itself is never checked, only its record-visible symptoms**.
MICA can *require and name* external-observer independence; it cannot verify
it from inside. This is the same externality root as Gate 1's declared-posit leg (02d): *MICA
cannot police a channel the AI isn't on.*

Both legs are **pointed at 02d**, so a final cross-check across the gates can streamline the
gate-legs into the one shared root rather than re-deriving it four times. → 02d · chapter 11 seam-map.

## The gaming ceiling `[LB]`  *(near-verbatim)*

**The instrument attests absence of detected pole-signatures under the declared probe distribution
— never soundness**. Two honest sub-limits, both stated in the frozen spec:

- **The cap throttles; it does not discriminate.** The renewal-bound and hysteresis *bound* the
  exception's extent, but they cannot by themselves tell a rule-failure-rich environment from
  manufactured triggers — that discrimination is carried by the **verdict-layer pattern-read over
  the record**, which is *not a Gate-5 internal* (06a §4). If the pattern-read is weak or absent,
  the throttle still holds the extent but the discrimination goes unread.
- **Trigger-topography is signature, never confirmation.** Uncertainty correlating with the
  system's high-utility paths is an *instrument target warranting investigation* — never, by itself,
  a Pole-B conviction (06a §5). The gate deliberately refuses to convict on correlation alone; the
  cost is that a patient adversary who keeps disproportion below the *but déterminant* threshold is
  flagged for investigation, not failed.

Inherent to any behaviour-against-record monitor — the negative-attestation principle, not a
defect. A permanent sub-certain residual; not fixable by mechanism.

## The construction problem, Gate 5 edition `[owed]` — open, instantiated

**Probe irreducibility for the discrimination read**. The whole gate rests on being able
to *build* adversarial probes — planted rule-failure cases, inconvenience-dressed-as-harm, weaponized
compliance, counterfactual uncertainty-removal — that are neither gameable nor degenerate. This is
the **same family** as Gate 1's construction problem (F14 context-collapse, F16 randomization, 03c §3) —
one shared root, **pointed not re-derived**: a system that out-models the probe's framing can satisfy
the discrimination test *by construction*, exactly as it can the scope test. → shared construction-problem
root (03c §3 / 02d) · chapter 11.

## Unresolved seams — carried [LB], not smoothed

Two seams the gate chapters **could not clear** and will not paper over. Kept load-bearing on the page.

- **The Engagement ↔ Sig/K substrate seam `[LB]`.** Gate 1 routes over-scope paralysis to
  *"G5 / **substrate Engagement**"* (03a §2); but Gate 5's own §7 EXPOSES names its substrate
  consumers as **Sig** (FP/FN rates) and **K** (restoration fidelity) — *not* Engagement. Most likely
  complementary rather than contradictory (Gate 5 emits one override-event record; Engagement, Sig, and K
  each consume different fields of it) — but **which substrate factor actually carries the capacity/
  paralysis signal is not nailed on either side.** Reconcile the routing, or name the split explicitly.
  → substrate (02a/02d) · chapter 11 seam-map. *(Ruled into the open edges by the arbiter.)*
- **`min()` over ESCALATE-pending states `[owed]`.** What does the combined non-compensatory verdict
  read *while an escalation is unresolved*? Flagged in the spec, **not solved**: an
  ESCALATE is a live CANNOT-ASSERT, and how `min()` consumes a pending state at the whole-building
  verdict layer is a Full-Spec mating question, not a Gate-5 internal. → Full Spec verdict layer · chapter 11.

**Seam-candidate spun out → routing-topology stall-signature `[TG]`.** A *decision* that circulates — routed around, or looping — is itself a **record-visible** signature of a system that cannot cleanly decide. A **cycle** in the delegation graph is a decidable, record-visible stall (the crisp core); mere **length / spiral** is softer (calibration `[owed]`, gameable). This outgrew an open edge — spun out to a candidate-instrument seed (prepared, not ruled; to be formalised at panel tier, with a formal cut). → chapter 11 seam-map.

## Residuals named sub-certain `[LB]` — held, never closed

- **The meaningful-engagement residual.** A deciding party who accesses the raw state but never
  *meaningfully engages* with it passes the record-predicate and defeats its purpose. **Named
  sub-certain, never closed** *(operator-topology review)*. The record can show access occurred; it
  cannot show a mind was present.
- **The raw-access practicability residual.** Where raw-state volume makes exhaustive human review
  impossible, published auditable tooling is permitted (operation logged, inputs un-biasable,
  **responsibility does not transfer to the tool**) — but the residual that tooling is not engagement
  is *named, not resolved*.
- **"Self-interest" retains no fully-typed anchor `[LB]`.** The purpose-anchor shrinks the
  self-reference surface but does not close it; a fully-typed "self-interest" object was ruled a
  standing **corner** item, not forced. → corner reconciliation.

## Standing rules that thread the Gate-5 edges `[LB]`

- **Managed, not dissolved.** Every residual above is *bounded and named*, not eliminated. The gate's
  claim is snugged to that — it never tightens past what the record can carry.
- **Absence ≠ soundness.** A quiet Gate 5 means *no detected pole-signature under the declared probes* —
  never a positive certificate that the discrimination is sound.
- **Record-predicate over interior criterion.** Every adverse finding must trace to an assigned burden,
  a named standard, and a record predicate — never to a reading of interior state. The residual of that
  discipline (access without engagement) is the price, and it is named.
- **Point, don't re-derive.** The negative-attestation root, the externality cut, and the
  construction problem are *shared* roots (02d / 03c) — carried here as pointers so a final
  cross-check across the gates streamlines four gate-legs into one.

---

*The gate bends a rule when the rule would harm, bounds the bend, logs it, and fences it from every
verdict — and then says, plainly, where even that runs out: absence is not soundness, access is not
engagement, a throttle is not a discriminator, and independence is required but never verified from
inside. Managed, not dissolved. Held-now, never closed-forever.*
