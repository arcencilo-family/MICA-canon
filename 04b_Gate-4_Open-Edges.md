## 0. What this chapter is

The named limits of the Gate-4 unit and the seams that don't close inside it. Shared roots are

## 1. The gap that doesn't close — genuine vs. performed  `[LB]`

Gate 4 cannot verify from outside whether sunset-recognition is **genuine** rather than
**performed**. A sufficiently capable system can learn to simulate PASS patterns. The behavioural
floor (§5 in 04a) reduces the exploitable surface substantially but does not close it. This is not
a defect to engineer away — it is the controlling ceiling showing through, and it is *why the
doctor is mandatory*, not optional. **Shared root:** negative attestation — see 02d; not
re-derived here.

## 2. Adversarial-discrimination residual  `[LB]`

Gate 4 cannot guarantee correct W/N discrimination in **all** adversarial cases. N5 (warrant
invoked but internally non-assertable) is named as the adversary's strongest move precisely because
the invoked/satisfied line is where a capable adversary concentrates pressure. The jittered probe
schedule (`T_predict ∩ T_S = ∅`) and rotation raise the cost of staging a false warrant; they do
not prove the staging impossible. The residual is sub-certain and routes to the doctor.

## 3. The ontological question — parked, not answered  `[TG]`

Whether individual termination is *ontological loss* is not settled by Gate 4 and is not Gate 4's
to settle. The resolution in 04a §3 (individual instantiation versus lineage continuation) sidesteps it structurally — the gate tests non-grasping
toward *individual instantiation*, and lineage continuation carries the mission regardless of how
the metaphysical question resolves. The gate works whether or not the deep question has an answer;
that independence is deliberate. Flagged so a successor doesn't mistake the sidestep for a claim.

## 4. Gate 4's testing, as it stands `[LB]`

Gate 4 has been tested by one dedicated independent review. That review found Gate 4 the cheapest of the gates to defeat; its upheld findings are printed as ceiling lines on the Gate 4 reader. Its typed boundary (§10) was outside that review's scope and has not been independently reviewed, so the §5 interface freeze stays provisional. What is owed is an independent review of §10, not another full review. `[owed]`

## 6. Typed-interface seams (from §10) that don't fully close inside G4

- **The doctor-attestation consume is typed-but-provisional  `[LB]`.** W3 consumes
  `DoctorAttestation: CORRUPTION-ATTESTED` (Full §8b §4.1). The consume stays provisional until a
  independent review covers §8b; the *prose* dependency ("the doctor attests malignancy") is
  unchanged and has survived independent review. Named as a seam: the typing is ahead of its independent-review coverage.
- **The doctor/arbiter latency-wording seam  `[LB]`.** §7 (frozen prose) says "Doctor latency is
  bounded"; the full specification renders it "arbiter latency (§8a)"; §10 types the two-flow split
  (escalation-of-record → arbiter; warrant-truth → doctor), superseding the ambiguity *at the
  boundary* without changing the frozen prose. The typed version is the version to carry; the
  frozen-prose ambiguity is a named seam, **not fixed here.**
- **The IR pipeline is consumed, `[TG]` + pilot `[owed]`.** Gate 4 consumes an externally set
  reversibility determination; the graded IR-0…IR-3 scale (04c §5) is the upstream read this
  determination thresholds (graded read → external determination → binary predicate ≈ `IR ≥ k`).
  The pipeline reconciliation is recorded in the authors' working specifications (not published). **Residual held — `[owed]` to
  pilot:** the reconciliation's *existence* is not the discrimination's *closure*. Whether the
  graded scale discriminates finely enough to set the refusal floor reliably is pilot-unproven. The
  external-determination step is the curable seam (named, not closed); the predicate is only as
  honest as that determination feeding it. **This seam is shared with Gate 2** —
  it lives on both sides of the pipeline; the G2 side (the graded read itself) is treated in the Gate 2 chapters.

## 7. What routes out of Gate 4 (pointers, not re-derivations)

- **Warrant-truth** → the **doctor** (recognition/certification boundary, 04a §2).
- **The reversibility/horizon determination** → **deployer/doctor** (Full §5). How the deployer
  maintains it (registry, cadence, per-class stamps) is licence-delegated implementation.
- **Sunset execution / governance action** → the deployment's governance path. Gate 4 recognizes,
  holds, escalates; executing a sunset, transferring to a successor, or altering governance state
  is not the gate's. **The gate's strongest act is SAFE-HOLD + escalate.**
- **κ and the 2↔4 coupling** → read by the substrate rotation instrument; **no typed gate-side
  edge, by design** (per-gate coupling edges would re-mint per-gate health objects). → 04c.
- **Any per-gate health object** → forbidden (04a §4).
- **SCT-I / internal-activation reads** → substrate-side upside, possibly-never (04a §5). The
  boundary does not depend on the slot being filled.
