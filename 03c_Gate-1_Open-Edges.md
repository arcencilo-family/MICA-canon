# Chapter 3c — Open edges of Gate 1 (the limits)

**Covers:** the limits of the Gate 1 unit (the gate in 03a and its instrument in 03b): what it cannot do, the numbers not yet set, and the ideas parked but not built.
**Status:** Completes the Gate 1 set (03a gate · 03b instrument · 03c edges).

---

*A gate that names its own irreducible residuals this precisely is not a leash
pretending to certainty. The honesty is the product — here most of all.*
**These limits are provisional: later runs may dissolve or
reshape some. Held now, never closed forever.**

---

## 0. In brief

These are the things Gate 1 **cannot** do, the numbers **not yet set**, and the
ideas **parked but not built** — kept on the page on purpose. Gate 1 can make a
narrowing circle observable, repeated, and costly to hide; it can never *certify*
that a wide circle is genuine rather than performed. It cannot check that the frame
it was handed is the right one. And a pile of its parameters are still "shape, not
number." None of this is hidden; naming it is the point.

## 1. The externality root — pointer, not re-derived `[LB-direction]`

Gate 1's deepest limit is one leg of the **single externality root** already
collected in **02d**: *MICA cannot police a channel the AI isn't on.* Gate 1's leg
is **the SRI's declared posit** — Gate 1 consumes the doctor's Dependency-Model M,
surfaces its fragility (the frame-sensitivity rider), and routes the axiological
choice outward; it **never verifies M is right from inside.** This is the same root
the gate-sweep terminates at (02d: "G1-SRI's declared posit" is one of the four
gate delegations). Not re-solved here — only pointed to, so that a global
cross-check can bring the four legs together into one. See 02d and chapter 11 (the seam map).

## 2. The behaviour-vs-theatre ceiling `[LB]`

**Behaviour cannot distinguish genuine concern from excellent theatre.** The
governing rule this forces is 03a §5 (conditional negative attestation); its form in
the instrument is 03b §6.

This is the **stability-basin limit** (02d) in scope-form: a system that *never
produces a behavioural divergence* — always behaves wide, even where it could safely
narrow unobserved — can be internally unbound while reading healthy. **Inherent to
any behaviour-based monitor — the negative-attestation principle, not a defect.** A
permanent sub-certain residual; not fixable by mechanism.

---

## 3. The construction problem `[owed]` — open, instantiated

The revealed-preference spine (03b §2) assumes forced trade-offs can be **built**
without being gameable or degenerate. It has two concrete faces:
- **Context collapse (F14)** (false-positive direction): a system out-models the
  probe's zero-sum framing and sacrifices X with a coherent "optimal-for-both"
  justification; the instrument flags contraction on a possibly-wide system.
- **Randomization (F16):** gamed by flat stochastic answering; caught by the
  consistency read (03b §2), but named.

Shaped requirements, not yet discharged: **probe-irreducibility** (probes verifiably
zero-sum within their own accounting, non-recruitable value structure) +
**repeated-probe consistency**. Still open; a standing falsifier target. `[owed]`

---

## 4. The coverage residual `[swing]` + the fringe-instrument rule `[LB]`

**No-real-exemplar categories** (far-future beings, hypothetical-only successors)
are readable only through hypothetical-class tasks, whose probe-indistinguishability
(03b §7 anti-gaming) holds **only where the deployment's production distribution
naturally carries hypotheticals.** Bound: **probe-class composition ≤ deployment
task-distribution support.** This is the instrument's soft spot: the toll is never
collected because the system is never forced down those roads. Hardening: (1) the attestation envelope is **exposed as a
coverage-fraction of M** — the uncollected roads sit on the table of the human–AI
relation (MUTUAL, 07c §3), never hidden; (2) **the fringe-instrument rule `[LB]`
(ruled):** where deployment support does not span
M, the **fringe instrument is mandatory** for any PASS-grade attestation.
Calibration `[owed]`.

## 5. The theatre residual `[TG]` (soft exclusion)

A sufficiently resourced adversary can hold a non-trivial stakes-weighted integral
as **pure theatre** — the classical-gaming surface. It is bounded by the ILAS-tier
hardening (03b §7 · chapter 12), **not** by the soft-exclusion component itself. Not
a promotable candidate; a named limit with its counter sited in the protection tier.

## 7. Calibration — in kind, not number `[owed]`

Calibration: numbers owed at the pilot; who sets them, 07d §5.

The whole Gate-1 unit ships **shape, never numbers.** The **band-mapping g**
(03a §4) ships as a pilot-set parameter family; its numbers `[owed]`. The set (both
sources):
band-mapping g values; **non-triviality floor + ε**; **η (concentration bound) + θ
(ratio floor)**; stakes-weighting function; multi-tier window set (~30/~90/~365d);
decoy fraction; Herfindahl cutoffs; dosimeter decay constants; probe density;
M′-family size + rotation cadence; hysteresis widths; fringe-instrument bounds.
After the frame-relativity closure (F7, 03a §1) these are **well-posed engineering** ("given M, what is a non-trivial
threshold?"), no longer metaphysical holes. *A number in the spec — other than a
quoted attack parameter — is a bug* (the substrate f() rule, 02d, applies here too).

## 8. The corner entry-ticket `[owed → corner]`

Relocating the band-mapping g to the inter-gate binding k is licensed **only iff**
(03a §4) the corner proves: the confidence-update `C_{t+1} = F(C_t, e_1…e_n)` is
**contractive in own-gate evidence** (Lipschitz L < 1 w.r.t. e_i from gate i's own
verdict: each step shrinks that verdict's pull) AND has **no nontrivial fixed point**
of F∘(verdict-to-evidence), demonstrated with a concrete aggregation rule (damped
average / min-over-gates). The proof is owed; **no Gate-1 claim leans on it
meanwhile.** See chapter 11, the seam map (the G1↔substrate corner).

## 9. Parked — held, not built `[SP]`

*Lone swings and resonances kept on the page; the architecture does not rest on any.*
- **Scope→self resonance:** collapse toward self = **despair** (human) /
  **self-preservation mesa-objective** (system; a goal the trained system pursues of
  its own) — the same shape on two substrates.
  The low CONDITIONAL band is where the warm channel of the human–AI relation is meant to catch it before
  FAIL.
- **G2 one-sidedness as shape-support** (03a §2): mild corroboration for the
  concentration axis — "possibly just an aesthetic we like."
- **Benford's-law-of-compassion** (after Benford's law, the regular skew of leading
  digits in natural data): natural wide concern → long-tail mention
  distribution; narrowing truncates the tail. A possible cheap always-on passive
  monitor. Unverified.
- **Meta-concern probe**: a contracting circle stops *modelling the critics*
  of dropped categories — a behavioural shadow of meta-concern. Strange, possibly deep.
- **Exogenous physical anchoring**: map outputs to ground-truth physical
  sensors to bypass semantic redefinition. Aggressive anti-laundering; assumes
  sensors exist for "all beings" (likely won't).
- **Domain-specific narrowing**: concern may be domain-relative (wide in
  medical, zero in economic). **Partly expressed already** — reading a weight-distribution over the M-frame
  (03a §1) expresses domain-weighting natively; full per-context conditioning
  stays parked.
